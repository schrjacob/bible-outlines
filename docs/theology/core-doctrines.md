## The Five Points of Calvinism (TULIP)

Chapell: *"Briefly define each of the 'five points' and cite two proof texts for each (both reference and full quotation). Some texts may support more than one point."* He also asks: *"Where did they come from?"*

**Origin.** After Jacobus Arminius died (1609), his followers presented five articles of protest, the **Remonstrance** (1610), against Reformed teaching on election, atonement, grace, and perseverance. The **Synod of Dort** (1618-19) answered with the **Canons of Dort**, which teach what came to be summarized as the "five points." The acronym TULIP is a later memory aid; the points are not the whole of Reformed theology, only its answer to five specific objections.

| | Point | In a sentence | Two proof texts (ESV) |
|---|---|---|---|
| **T** | **Total depravity** | Sin has corrupted every part of a person (mind, will, affections), so that apart from grace no one is able or willing to come to God. | **Gen. 6:5:** "The LORD saw that the wickedness of man was great in the earth, and that every intention of the thoughts of his heart was only evil continually."<br>**Rom. 3:10-12:** "None is righteous, no, not one; no one understands; no one seeks for God. All have turned aside; together they have become worthless; no one does good, not even one." |
| **U** | **Unconditional election** | Before the world began God chose a people in Christ, not because of anything he foresaw in them (faith or works), but solely out of his sovereign good pleasure. | **Eph. 1:4:** "even as he chose us in him before the foundation of the world, that we should be holy and blameless before him."<br>**Rom. 9:15-16:** "For he says to Moses, 'I will have mercy on whom I have mercy, and I will have compassion on whom I have compassion.' So then it depends not on human will or exertion, but on God, who has mercy." |
| **L** | **Limited atonement** (particular redemption) | Christ died to secure the salvation of the elect: his death actually redeems the people the Father gave him, not merely making salvation possible for all. | **John 10:11:** "I am the good shepherd. The good shepherd lays down his life for the sheep."<br>**Matt. 1:21:** "She will bear a son, and you shall call his name Jesus, for he will save his people from their sins." |
| **I** | **Irresistible grace** (effectual calling) | The Spirit's inward call always overcomes the sinner's resistance and brings the elect to faith; he does not coerce the will but changes it. | **John 6:37:** "All that the Father gives me will come to me, and whoever comes to me I will never cast out."<br>**Ezek. 36:26-27:** "And I will give you a new heart, and a new spirit I will put within you. And I will remove the heart of stone from your flesh and give you a heart of flesh. And I will put my Spirit within you, and cause you to walk in my statutes and be careful to obey my rules." |
| **P** | **Perseverance of the saints** | Those whom God has chosen, called, and justified will persevere in faith to the end, because God preserves them. | **John 10:28-29:** "I give them eternal life, and they will never perish, and no one will snatch them out of my hand. My Father, who has given them to me, is greater than all, and no one can snatch them out of the Father's hand."<br>**Phil. 1:6:** "And I am sure of this, that he who began a good work in you will bring it to completion at the day of Jesus Christ." |

Chapell's own list of texts also includes: **T:** Jer. 17:9; Eph. 2:1-3; Rom. 8:7-8 · **U:** John 6:37, 39; Eph. 1:11; 2:8-10; Titus 3:5 · **L:** John 6:37, 44; 10:11, 14-16; Rom. 8:29-30 · **I:** John 6:37, 44, 65; 10:16; Acts 11:18; 13:48; Phil. 2:11-13 · **P:** John 6:44; Rom. 8:29-30; Phil. 1:6.

!!! tip "Say it right"
    The Reformed do not teach that the five points are five separate doctrines. They are one doctrine, **salvation is of the Lord** (Jonah 2:9), viewed from five sides. Each depends on the others: if sinners are dead (T), only God's choice (U), Christ's actual saving work (L), and the Spirit's effectual call (I) can save them, and he finishes what he begins (P).

## The Ordo Salutis (Order of Salvation)

Chapell: *"List clearly the parts of the ordo as you understand it and cite relevant proof texts (both reference and full quotation) that help establish the order."* The *ordo salutis* is the **logical** order (not necessarily the time order) in which the Holy Spirit applies Christ's redemption to the elect.

| # | Step | Meaning | Key text |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Election** (predestination) | God's choice, before time, of those he would save | Eph. 1:4-5; Rom. 8:29 |
| 2 | **Effectual calling** | The Spirit's inward summons that brings a person to Christ | Rom. 8:30; 1 Cor. 1:9; John 6:44 |
| 3 | **Regeneration** | New birth: the Spirit makes the dead sinner alive, a new heart | John 3:3-8; Eph. 2:4-5; Titus 3:5 |
| 4 | **Faith and repentance** (conversion) | The sinner's response, itself a gift: trusting Christ and turning from sin | Eph. 2:8-9; Acts 11:18; 2 Tim. 2:25 |
| 5 | **Justification** | God declares the believing sinner righteous because of Christ's righteousness credited to him | Rom. 3:28; 5:1; 8:30 |
| 6 | **Adoption** | God receives the justified as his children, with all a son's privileges | John 1:12; Rom. 8:15; Gal. 4:4-7 |
| 7 | **Sanctification** | The Spirit's lifelong work of making the believer holy | Rom. 6:22; 1 Thess. 5:23; Phil. 2:12-13 |
| 8 | **Perseverance** | God keeps the believer in faith to the end | John 10:27-29; Phil. 1:6 |
| 9 | **Glorification** | Resurrection and final perfection | Rom. 8:30; Phil. 3:20-21; 1 John 3:2 |

Some theologians treat regeneration, faith, and repentance a little differently (for example, regeneration and effectual calling together, or conversion as one act with two sides). Know your order and the reasons for it. The **golden chain** of Rom. 8:29-30 gives the skeleton; John 6 and Eph. 2 show that the new birth comes before faith.

Chapell's suggested proof texts (quote at least these):

- **Rom. 8:29-30:** "For those whom he foreknew he also predestined to be conformed to the image of his Son, in order that he might be the firstborn among many brothers. And those whom he predestined he also called, and those whom he called he also justified, and those whom he justified he also glorified."
- **Rom. 5:1:** "Therefore, since we have been justified by faith, we have peace with God through our Lord Jesus Christ."
- **Rom. 6:22:** "But now that you have been set free from sin and have become slaves of God, the fruit you get leads to sanctification and its end, eternal life."
- **Eph. 2:8-10:** "For by grace you have been saved through faith. And this is not your own doing; it is the gift of God, not a result of works, so that no one may boast. For we are his workmanship, created in Christ Jesus for good works, which God prepared beforehand, that we should walk in them."
- **Titus 3:5-7:** "He saved us, not because of works done by us in righteousness, but according to his own mercy, by the washing of regeneration and renewal of the Holy Spirit, whom he poured out on us richly through Jesus Christ our Savior, so that being justified by his grace we might become heirs according to the hope of eternal life."
- **John 6:37, 39-40, 44:** "All that the Father gives me will come to me, and whoever comes to me I will never cast out." "...that I should lose nothing of all that he has given me, but raise it up on the last day." "No one can come to me unless the Father who sent me draws him. And I will raise him up on the last day."
- **John 10:26-29:** "...but you do not believe because you are not among my sheep. My sheep hear my voice, and I know them, and they follow me. I give them eternal life, and they will never perish, and no one will snatch them out of my hand."

## A Gospel Outline

Chapell: *"Simply, briefly and clearly explain the gospel to an unbeliever and cite proof texts (both reference and full quotation) for each of your main points."* A simple, memorable shape: **God, man, Christ, response.**

| Point | What to say | Proof text (ESV) |
|---|---|---|
| **1. God is holy and he made us for himself** | The one true God made everything and everyone to know, love, and glorify him; he is perfectly holy and just. | **Isa. 6:3:** "Holy, holy, holy is the LORD of hosts; the whole earth is full of his glory!" |
| **2. We are sinners, cut off from God** | Everyone has broken God's law and deserves his judgment; we cannot fix this ourselves. | **Rom. 3:23:** "For all have sinned and fall short of the glory of God."<br>**Rom. 6:23:** "For the wages of sin is death, but the free gift of God is eternal life in Christ Jesus our Lord." |
| **3. Christ died and rose for sinners** | God the Son became man, lived the perfect life we have not lived, died in the place of sinners, and rose again. | **Rom. 5:8:** "But God shows his love for us in that while we were still sinners, Christ died for us."<br>**1 Cor. 15:3-4:** "For I delivered to you as of first importance what I also received: that Christ died for our sins in accordance with the Scriptures, that he was buried, that he was raised on the third day in accordance with the Scriptures." |
| **4. We must repent and believe** | Turn from sin and trust Christ alone; salvation is a free gift received by faith, not earned. | **Mark 1:15:** "The time is fulfilled, and the kingdom of God is at hand; repent and believe in the gospel."<br>**Rom. 10:9:** "If you confess with your mouth that Jesus is Lord and believe in your heart that God raised him from the dead, you will be saved."<br>**Acts 16:31:** "Believe in the Lord Jesus, and you will be saved, you and your household." |

Be ready to add: what a person will gain (forgiveness, adoption, the Spirit, eternal life); that following Christ costs something (Luke 9:23); and that you are inviting the person to come, not demanding a performance.
