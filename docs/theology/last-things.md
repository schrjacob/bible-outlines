# The Last Things

<!-- cols:1 continuous -->

Chapell, theology section J. Standards: **WCF 32-33; WLC 84-85, 87-90; WSC 37-38** (Chapell assigns **WSC 37-38** for memorization). The **Tier** column ranks each question (1 = know cold, 2 = second pass, 3 = if time allows).

!!! tip "Memorize WSC 37-38"
    **37.** *What benefits do believers receive from Christ at death?* "The souls of believers are at their death made perfect in holiness, and do immediately pass into glory; and their bodies, being still united to Christ, do rest in their graves till the resurrection."
    **38.** *What benefits do believers receive from Christ at the resurrection?* "At the resurrection, believers, being raised up in glory, shall be openly acknowledged and acquitted in the day of judgment, and made perfectly blessed in the full enjoying of God to all eternity."

| Tier | Question | Answer and proofs |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | What happens to believers at death? Defend your answer with Scripture proofs. | Their **souls are made perfect in holiness and immediately enter the presence of Christ in glory**, while their **bodies**, still united to Christ, rest in the grave until the resurrection (WCF 32.1; WSC 37). Luke 23:43 ("today you will be with me in Paradise"); 2 Cor. 5:8; Phil. 1:21-23; Heb. 12:23; Rev. 6:9-11; 14:13. No purgatory, no soul sleep. |
| 1 | What happens to unbelievers at death? Defend your answer with Scripture proofs. | Their souls are cast into **hell**, "where they remain in torments and utter darkness, reserved to the judgment of the great day" (WCF 32.1). Luke 16:22-26 (the rich man); 2 Pet. 2:9; Jude 6-7; Heb. 9:27. |
| 1 | Who will be raised to life at the last day? | **All the dead**, both the just and the unjust (WCF 32.2-3; Acts 24:15; John 5:28-29; Dan. 12:2; Rev. 20:12-13), with the same bodies, raised up by the power of Christ. The **righteous** are raised to honor, with bodies like Christ's glorious body (Phil. 3:21; 1 Cor. 15:42-44, 51-52; 1 Thess. 4:16-17); the **wicked** to dishonor. |
| 1 | Who will be judged at the last day? Defend your answer with Scripture proofs. | **All people** and all angels (2 Cor. 5:10; Rom. 14:10-12; Matt. 25:31-46; Rev. 20:11-15; Eccl. 12:14; 2 Pet. 2:4; Jude 6). For believers, the judgment will **acquit** them and reveal their works as evidence and reward, not as the ground of condemnation (Rom. 8:1; 1 Cor. 3:12-15; 2 Cor. 5:10). |
| 1 | By whom will they be judged? Defend your answer with Scripture proofs. | **Jesus Christ**, appointed by the Father as judge, "all power and judgment" being given to him (WCF 33.1; John 5:22, 27; Acts 10:42; 17:31; Matt. 25:31-32; 2 Tim. 4:1; Rom. 2:16). The saints will share in judging (1 Cor. 6:2-3). |
| 1 | Will any receive a second chance? Defend your answer with Scripture proofs. | **No**: "it is appointed for man to die once, and after that comes judgment" (Heb. 9:27); the chasm of Luke 16:26 cannot be crossed; now is the day of salvation (2 Cor. 6:2; Matt. 25:10-12). The wicked are cast into everlasting torments (WCF 33.2). Universalism and post-mortem salvation contradict these texts; 1 Pet. 3:19-20 and 4:6 are not about a second chance. |
| 1 | What practical use is the doctrine of the final judgment? Include Scripture proofs. | WCF 33.3: it **deters men from sin** and gives **comfort to the godly in adversity**. It motivates **holiness** (2 Pet. 3:11-14; 2 Cor. 5:9-11), **evangelism** (2 Cor. 5:11), **patience** under injustice (Rom. 12:19; 2 Thess. 1:5-10), and **watchfulness**, since the day is unknown (Matt. 24:42-44; 25:13). The believer's assurance rests on Christ his advocate and judge (Rom. 8:33-34; 1 John 2:1; 4:17). |
| 1 | When will Christ return? Defend your answer with Scripture proofs. | **No one knows the day or hour** (Matt. 24:36, 42-44; 25:13; Acts 1:7; 1 Thess. 5:1-3); Christ would have it unknown so that men shake off carnal security and be always watchful (WCF 33.3). His return will be **personal, visible, bodily, and sudden** (Acts 1:11; Matt. 24:27, 30; 1 Thess. 4:16-17; Rev. 1:7). Date-setting is forbidden by Christ's own words. |
| 1 | What will heaven be like? | **Presence with God and Christ forever** (Rev. 21:3-4; 22:1-5; 1 Thess. 4:17; Phil. 1:23); perfect **holiness**; no sin, sorrow, or death (Rev. 21:4); **fullness of joy** (Ps. 16:11); the **vision of God** (1 John 3:2; Matt. 5:8; 1 Cor. 13:12); **resurrected bodies** (1 Cor. 15); and the **new heavens and new earth** (Isa. 65:17; 2 Pet. 3:13; Rev. 21:1; Rom. 8:19-23), with worship, service, and rest (Rev. 7:15; Heb. 4:9). Degrees of reward (Dan. 12:3; 1 Cor. 3:14; Luke 19:17), all perfectly blessed (WSC 38). |
| 1 | Is there a hell? Defend your answer with Scripture proofs. | **Yes.** Jesus taught it more than anyone: Matt. 5:29-30; 10:28; 18:8-9; 25:41, 46; Mark 9:43-48; Luke 16:19-31. Hell is **conscious, eternal punishment**, separation from the favorable presence of God (2 Thess. 1:7-9; Rev. 14:10-11; 20:10-15; Dan. 12:2; Isa. 66:24), with degrees of punishment (Luke 12:47-48; Matt. 11:22-24). WCF 32.1; 33.2. It is just, because sin is an offense against an infinitely holy God. |
| 2 | What is "annihilationism?" Evaluate it biblically. | The view (also called **conditional immortality**) that the wicked are finally **destroyed**: they cease to exist rather than suffer endless torment (Edward Fudge; John Stott was sympathetic), based on "destruction" texts (Matt. 10:28; Phil. 3:19; 2 Thess. 1:9; Mal. 4:1-3). **Critique:** "eternal punishment" is parallel to "eternal life" in Matt. 25:46; the torment is "day and night forever and ever" (Rev. 14:11; 20:10); "their worm does not die" (Mark 9:48); the rich man is conscious (Luke 16:23-24); "destruction" in Scripture means **ruin**, not extinction; the Standards teach "eternal torments" (WCF 33.2). |
| 1 | What is your view of the millennium? Briefly defend your view from Scripture. | State **your** view; the Standards do **not** require one, and the PCA includes officers of all three main views. **Amillennial:** the "thousand years" of Rev. 20:1-6 is the present age between Christ's first and second comings; Satan is bound (Matt. 12:29; Luke 10:18; John 12:31; Col. 2:15) and Christ reigns now from heaven (1 Cor. 15:20-28). **Postmillennial:** the gospel will succeed so that the world is largely Christianized before Christ's return (Ps. 22:27; Isa. 11:9; Matt. 28:18-20). **Premillennial:** Christ returns **before** a literal thousand-year reign on earth (historic premillennialism; dispensational premillennialism adds a pretribulational rapture and a separate plan for national Israel, which many Reformed regard as in tension with covenant theology's one people of God). Know the passages for your view (Rev. 20:1-10; Isa. 11; Rom. 11:25-27; 1 Cor. 15:22-26; Matt. 24-25), and be ready to answer questions about the opposite views. |
