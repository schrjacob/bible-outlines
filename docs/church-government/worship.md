# Worship

<!-- cols:1 continuous -->

Chapell, church government section J, "The Directory for Worship" (chapters 47-63). This is the third part of the BCO. For the **theology** of worship (the regulative principle and the elements), see [The Christian Life](../theology/christian-life.md); for the **sacraments**, see [Sacraments](../sacraments/index.md). The **Tier** column ranks each question (1 = know cold, 2 = second pass, 3 = if time allows). Check specifics against the current BCO (see the warning on the [overview](index.md)).

| Tier | Question | Answer and proofs |
|---|---|---|
| 2 | What parts of the Directory for Worship have constitutional authority (if any)? | The Directory for Worship is **guidance for the conduct of worship**, "a directory," not a set of rules like the Form of Government and Rules of Discipline; **only the portions the BCO itself designates as binding** carry constitutional authority (the **principles** of worship, in particular the regulative principle of BCO 47, and the parts that restate the Standards). **Look up your current BCO's own statement of which chapters bind** and be ready to say it; examiners ask this. |
| 1 | What rule guides public worship? | The **regulative principle**: worship is to be conducted **only as God has appointed in his Word** (BCO 47; WCF 21.1; Lev. 10:1-3; Deut. 12:32; Matt. 15:9; John 4:23-24). Circumstances (time, place, order) are governed by the general rules of Scripture and the church's good order (WCF 1.6). |
| 1 | What is the purpose of public worship? | To **glorify God** and to **edify his people**: to meet with God and render him praise and adoration, to **hear his Word**, to **receive his sacraments**, to **offer prayers and offerings**, and so to **build up the body** (BCO 47; Ps. 29:2; 95:6; Acts 2:42; 1 Cor. 14:26; Heb. 10:24-25). |
| 1 | What are the proper elements of worship? | The **reading of Scripture**, **preaching** the Word, **prayer** (adoration, confession, thanksgiving, supplication, intercession), the **singing of psalms, hymns, and spiritual songs**, the **administration of the sacraments** (baptism and the Lord's Supper), **offerings**, and the **blessing**; also occasional elements such as **oaths and vows**, **fasting**, and **thanksgiving** (BCO 47-49; WCF 21.3-5; Acts 2:42; Col. 3:16; 1 Cor. 16:2). |
| 1 | In brief, how ought we to keep the Lord's Day? | As **the Christian Sabbath**: set apart from **worldly employment and recreation** (except **works of necessity and mercy**), spent in **public and private worship** and in the **duties of devotion, mercy, and rest** (BCO 48; WCF 21.7-8; Ex. 20:8-11; Isa. 58:13-14; Mark 2:27; Acts 20:7; Rev. 1:10). See [The Christian Life](../theology/christian-life.md) for the Westminster and Continental views. |
| 1 | Who is responsible for deciding who preaches in a church? | The **session**, which has the oversight of the **pulpit**: it decides who may preach, and the **pastor** has the **primary responsibility** for the preaching, subject to the session's oversight (BCO 53; verify). |
| 1 | What vows do parents make at the baptism of their children? (Summarize.) | The parents (or the parent or parents who present the child) are asked to **acknowledge** the child's **need of the cleansing blood of Christ and the renewing of the Holy Spirit**; to **claim God's covenant promises** for the child and **look in faith to Christ** for his salvation as for their own; and to **dedicate the child to God**, promising to **set a godly example, pray with and for him, teach him the doctrines of our holy religion**, and use all the means God has appointed to bring him up in the **nurture and admonition of the Lord**. The **congregation** is also asked to **promise** its support and care (BCO 56; verify the current wording). |
| 1 | Who admits people to the Lord's Supper? What vows are made? | The **session** admits people to the Lord's Supper, after **examining** them on their **knowledge** and **profession of faith** (BCO 57; 1 Cor. 11:27-29). The new communicant, in substance, is asked to **acknowledge** that he is a **sinner** under God's judgment; to **believe** in Jesus Christ, **receiving and resting on him alone** for salvation; to **repent** and promise to **live as a follower of Christ**; to **submit to the government and discipline** of the church; and to **seek its peace, unity, and purity** (BCO 57; verify the current vows). The vows of **profession of faith** are made before the congregation. |
