# Worship

<!-- cols:1 continuous -->

Chapell, church government section J, "The Directory for Worship" (chapters 47-63). This is the third part of the BCO. For the **theology** of worship (the regulative principle and the elements), see [The Christian Life](../theology/christian-life.md); for the **sacraments**, see [Sacraments](../sacraments/index.md). The **Tier** column ranks each question (1 = know cold, 2 = second pass, 3 = if time allows). Checked against the BCO text through the 2026 General Assembly (see the note on the [overview](index.md)).

| Tier | Question | Answer and proofs |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | What parts of the Directory for Worship have constitutional authority (if any)? | The BCO's own preface to the Directory states: "The Directory for Worship is an **approved guide** and should be taken seriously as the mind of the Church agreeable to the Standards. However, it **does not have the force of law** and is not to be considered obligatory in all its parts. **BCO 56, 57 and 58** (baptism, admission to sealing ordinances, and the Lord's Supper) have been given **full constitutional authority** by the Eleventh General Assembly after approval by two-thirds of the presbyteries. **BCO 59-3** (marriage as only between one man and one woman) has been given full constitutional authority by the Forty-seventh General Assembly." |
| 1 | What rule guides public worship? | "Since the Holy Scriptures are the only infallible rule of faith and practice, the **principles of public worship must be derived from the Bible, and from no other source**. The Scriptures forbid the worshipping of God by images, or in any other way not appointed in His Word" (BCO 47-1; the **regulative principle**; WCF 21.1; Lev. 10:1-3; Deut. 12:32; Matt. 15:9). Christ "has prescribed no fixed forms," giving "a large measure of liberty," but "all things must be done decently and in order" (47-6; 1 Cor. 14:40). |
| 1 | What is the purpose of public worship? | "The end of public worship is **the glory of God**" (BCO 47-3). It is "not merely a gathering of God's children with each other, but before all else, a **meeting of the triune God with His chosen people**" (47-2). It aims at "the **building of Christ's Church** by the perfecting of the saints and the addition to its membership of such as are being saved," and teaches Christians "to serve God all the days of the week" (47-3; 1 Cor. 10:31). It must be Christian (through Christ the Mediator, 47-4), in spirit and truth (47-5; John 4:24), and covenant children should be present so far as possible (47-7). |
| 1 | What are the proper elements of worship? | BCO 47-9: "the Bible teaches that the following are proper elements of worship service: **reading of Holy Scripture, singing of psalms and hymns, the offering of prayer, the preaching of the Word, the presentation of offerings, confessing the faith and observing the Sacraments; and on special occasions taking oaths**." (See chapters 50-55 for each.) |
| 1 | In brief, how ought we to keep the Lord's Day? | BCO 48: "The whole day is to be kept holy to the Lord; and to be employed in the public and private exercises of religion. Therefore, it is requisite, that there be a **holy resting, all the day, from unnecessary labors; and an abstaining from those recreations which may be lawful on other days; and also, as much as possible, from worldly thoughts and conversation**" (48-4). It is the first day, sanctified by Christ's resurrection (48-2). Prepare beforehand and lay aside worldly business (48-3); spend the time not in public worship in prayer, devotional reading, Scripture study, catechizing, religious conversation, psalm-singing, visiting the sick, relieving the poor, and "such like duties of piety, charity, and mercy" (48-7). See WSC 57-62 and [The Christian Life](../theology/christian-life.md). |
| 1 | Who is responsible for deciding who preaches in a church? | The **Session**: "No person should be invited to preach in any of the churches under our care **without the consent of the Session**" (BCO 53-6). The session is charged "to ensure that the Word of God is preached only by such men as are sufficiently qualified" (12-5.e; 53-2 is cited there). Regular preaching in a PCA pulpit requires **licensure** by presbytery (19-1). |
| 1 | What vows do parents make at the baptism of their children? (Summarize.) | BCO 56-5: after the covenant promises are read (Acts 2:39; Gen. 17:7; Acts 16:31), the minister asks the parent(s): (1) **Do you acknowledge your child's need of the cleansing blood of Jesus Christ, and the renewing grace of the Holy Spirit?** (2) **Do you claim God's covenant promises in his behalf**, and look in faith to Christ for his salvation as for your own? (3) **Do you now unreservedly dedicate your child to God**, and promise, in humble reliance upon divine grace, to set a **godly example**, **pray with and for him**, **teach him the doctrines of our holy religion**, and strive by all the means of God's appointment to bring him up **in the nurture and admonition of the Lord**? The **congregation** may optionally be asked whether it undertakes to assist the parents in the child's Christian nurture. Baptism is administered by pouring or sprinkling water, in the name of the Father, Son, and Holy Spirit, by a **minister** and in the presence of the congregation under the session's supervision (56-1, 56-2, 56-6). |
| 1 | Who admits people to the Lord's Supper? What vows are made? | The **Session**: it is "the prudence of the Session, whose office it is to judge, after careful examination, the qualifications of those who apply for admission to sealing ordinances" (BCO 57-2). Baptized persons admitted to the Supper are recommended to make a **public profession of faith** before the congregation (57-4). Those making a public profession are asked (57-5): (1) Do you **acknowledge yourselves to be sinners** in the sight of God, justly deserving his displeasure, and without hope save in his sovereign mercy? (2) Do you **believe in the Lord Jesus Christ as the Son of God, and Savior of sinners**, and receive and rest upon him alone for salvation as offered in the Gospel? (3) Do you resolve and promise, in reliance on the Holy Spirit, to **endeavor to live as becomes the followers of Christ**? (4) Do you promise to **support the Church in its worship and work**? (5) Do you **submit yourselves to the government and discipline of the Church**, and promise to study its purity and peace? (Persons received by transfer give a testimony of Christian experience to the session, 57-6.) |
| 2 | How often is the Lord's Supper observed, and who may be invited to it? | "To be observed **frequently**; the stated times to be determined by the Session of each congregation" (BCO 58-1). "**The ignorant and scandalous are not to be admitted**" (58-2). Before the observance the minister, at the Session's discretion, may invite **either** "all those who profess the true religion, and are communicants in good standing in any evangelical church," **or** those approved by the Session after they have indicated a desire to participate (58-4). It is proper to give a special invitation to non-communicants to remain during the service (58-4). Public notice should be given at least the Sabbath before (58-3). |
| 3 | What other occasions does the Directory cover? | **Marriage** (59: one man and one woman, 59-3; marry "in the Lord"; consent; publication), **visitation of the sick** (60), **burial** (61), **days of fasting and thanksgiving** (62), and **Christian life in the home**, including family and secret worship (63). It also covers the order of worship, Scripture reading from "a good translation, not a paraphrase" (50-3), the singing of psalms and hymns (51; the session oversees leadership), prayer (52), preaching (53), offerings, including the tithe as a standard (54-1), and confessing the faith in creeds (55-1). |
