## Names of Jesus

- **Jesus** = Joshua ("the LORD saves," Matt. 1:21)
- **Christ** = Messiah, "Anointed One": refers to the anointing of a king (also prophet and priest)
- **Son of David** = Messiah: refers to the ancestry of the promised King (2 Sam. 7)
- **King of the Jews / King of Israel** = Messiah (the former used by non-Jews)
- **Son of God** = Messiah during his lifetime (Matt. 26:63; John 1:49; cf. Ps. 2:7); the eternal second person of the Trinity, declared with power by the resurrection (Rom. 1:4)
- **Son of Man** = the *universal* King of Daniel 7:13-14
    - Jesus' own favorite name for himself
    - Used both of his suffering and of his return in glory
- **Lord** (*kyrios*) = a range of meanings, from "sir" to deity (LXX for YHWH; Phil. 2:11)
- **Lamb of God** = the sacrificial substitute who takes away sin (John 1:29; Isa. 53:7; Ex. 12)
- **I AM** = the divine name of Ex. 3:14 (John 8:58)

## Jesus' Teaching

- **Key themes:** the kingdom of God, the fatherhood of God, the return of Christ, the cross
- **Definition of the kingdom:** "the rule and realm of God"
- **Importance of the kingdom:** the key to the renewal of us and of the world (sin, which spoils everything, is an authority problem)
- **Mystery of the kingdom:** already, but not yet (present and still future); the King comes "under cover"
- **Parable:** a story of ordinary life with a larger point
- **Main subjects of the parables:** the kingdom of God and the return of Christ
- **Teaching about Christ's return.** It will be:
    - for judgment (Matt. 25:31-46)
    - unexpected, "sudden" (Matt. 24:36-44)
    - unmistakable (Matt. 24:27, 30)
    - linked with the destruction of Jerusalem (Matt. 24:2; Luke 21:20)
    - always taught with a practical, not speculative, focus: "stay awake" (Matt. 24:42-44)
- **Miracles** attest his revelation and identity (John 20:30-31; Acts 2:22; Heb. 2:3-4) and are signs of the kingdom's arrival (Matt. 12:28)

## Three Parables (and More)

!!! example "Know these three well"
    1. **The Sower** (Matt. 13:1-23; Mark 4:1-20; Luke 8:4-15): the word of the kingdom meets four kinds of soil. The kingdom grows through the Word, but people receive it in different ways; only the "good soil" bears fruit.
    2. **The Good Samaritan** (Luke 10:25-37): answers "Who is my neighbor?" The despised Samaritan shows mercy where religious leaders passed by. Love crosses every boundary ("Go and do likewise").
    3. **The Prodigal Son** (Luke 15:11-32): the father runs to welcome his repentant son. God rejoices over sinners who repent; the elder brother exposes the self-righteousness of the Pharisees.

## Three Miracles (and More)

!!! example "Know these three well"
    1. **Water into wine** at Cana (John 2:1-11): the first sign. It "manifested his glory," and the new wine of the kingdom replaces the water of Jewish purification.
    2. **Feeding of the 5,000** (Matt. 14; Mark 6; Luke 9; John 6): the only miracle in all four Gospels. Jesus is the true bread from heaven (John 6:35), the new Moses providing manna.
    3. **Raising of Lazarus** (John 11): "I am the resurrection and the life." This seventh sign shows his power over death and precipitates the plot to kill him.
