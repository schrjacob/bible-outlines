# Pauline Epistles

!!! info "Exam level"
    **Outlines:** Romans, 1-2 Corinthians, Galatians, Ephesians, Philippians, Colossians. Romans gets the most detail: it is the backbone of the theology exam too.
    **Summaries:** 1-2 Thessalonians, 1-2 Timothy, Titus, Philemon. See [Life of Paul](paul.md) for when and where each was written.

## Romans (16)

*The Righteousness of God Revealed in the Gospel*

**Theme (1:16-17):** "For I am not ashamed of the gospel, for it is the power of God for salvation to everyone who believes, to the Jew first and also to the Greek. For in it the righteousness of God is revealed from faith for faith, as it is written, 'The righteous shall live by faith.'"

Memory aid: **Sin, Salvation, Sanctification, Sovereignty, Service.**

- **1:1-17** Introduction; theme
- **1:18-3:20** **Sin:** the whole world condemned (righteousness *needed*)
    - **1:18-32** Gentiles suppress the truth revealed in creation; "God gave them up"
    - **2:1-3:8** Jews are judged by the law they possess
    - **3:9-20** All are under sin: "None is righteous, no, not one"
- **3:21-5:21** **Salvation:** justification by faith (righteousness *imputed*)
    - **3:21-31** Justified freely by grace through faith; Christ a propitiation (3:23-26)
    - **4** Abraham (and David) justified by faith apart from works (Gen. 15:6)
    - **5:1-11** Fruits of justification: peace, access, hope ("while we were still sinners, Christ died for us," 5:8)
    - **5:12-21** Adam and Christ: two federal heads; imputation
- **6-8** **Sanctification:** life in Christ (righteousness *imparted*)
    - **6** Dead to sin, alive to God: union with Christ in baptism; "the wages of sin is death" (6:23)
    - **7** Freed from the law; the struggle with indwelling sin (7:14-25)
    - **8** Life in the Spirit: "no condemnation" (8:1); adoption (8:15); "all things work together for good" (8:28); the golden chain (8:29-30); nothing can separate us (8:31-39)
- **9-11** **Sovereignty:** God's righteousness vindicated in Israel's unbelief
    - **9** Election (Jacob and Esau; the potter and the clay)
    - **10** Israel's responsibility; "if you confess with your mouth... and believe in your heart" (10:9); "how are they to hear without someone preaching?" (10:14-17)
    - **11** A remnant; the olive tree; "all Israel will be saved"; doxology (11:33-36)
- **12:1-15:13** **Service:** righteousness practiced
    - **12** Living sacrifices (12:1-2); gifts in the body; genuine love
    - **13** Submission to civil government (13:1-7); love fulfills the law (13:8-10)
    - **14:1-15:13** The weak and the strong: Christian liberty
- **15:14-16:27** Conclusion: Paul's plans (Jerusalem, Rome, Spain); greetings (Phoebe, Priscilla and Aquila); doxology

## 1 Corinthians (16)

*Correcting a Divided, Worldly Church by the Gospel of the Cross*

Written from Ephesus (c. AD 54-55) in response to reports from "Chloe's people" (1:11) and a letter from the church (7:1, "Now concerning...").

- **1-4** Divisions in the church
    - "I follow Paul... Apollos... Cephas... Christ" (1:12)
    - The foolishness of the cross is God's wisdom (1:18-2:16)
    - "I planted, Apollos watered, but God gave the growth" (3:6); the church as God's temple (3:16)
- **5-6** Moral disorders
    - **5** Incest; church discipline ("purge the evil person")
    - **6:1-11** Lawsuits among believers
    - **6:12-20** Sexual immorality; the body as a temple of the Holy Spirit (6:19-20)
- **7** Marriage, singleness, and divorce
- **8-10** Food offered to idols: Christian liberty and love
    - **9** Paul's apostolic rights given up for the gospel ("all things to all people")
    - **10** Israel's example as a warning; "do all to the glory of God" (10:31)
- **11-14** Disorders in worship
    - **11** Head coverings; the Lord's Supper (11:23-26) and self-examination
    - **12** Spiritual gifts: one body, many members
    - **13** **The love chapter**
    - **14** Prophecy and tongues; "all things... decently and in order" (14:40)
- **15** The resurrection: Christ's (the gospel summary, 15:3-8) and ours (the spiritual body, 15:35-58)
- **16** The collection; travel plans; greetings

## 2 Corinthians (13)

*Power in Weakness: Paul's Defense of His Ministry*

Written from Macedonia (c. AD 55-56) after a painful visit and a "severe letter."

- **1-7** Paul's ministry explained
    - **1** The God of all comfort; Paul's change of travel plans
    - **2** Forgive and restore the repentant offender
    - **3** Ministers of a new covenant: glory greater than Moses'
    - **4** "We have this treasure in jars of clay" (4:7)
    - **5** Our heavenly dwelling; "a new creation" (5:17); the ministry of reconciliation (5:18-21)
    - **6-7** Do not be unequally yoked; godly grief produces repentance (7:10)
- **8-9** The collection for the Jerusalem saints: Christ became poor for us (8:9); "God loves a cheerful giver" (9:7)
- **10-13** Paul's apostolic authority defended against the "super-apostles"
    - Boasting in weakness; Paul's sufferings (11)
    - Caught up to the third heaven; the thorn in the flesh: "My grace is sufficient for you, for my power is made perfect in weakness" (12:9)

## Galatians (6)

*Justification by Faith Alone; Freedom in Christ*

Written to the churches of Galatia against Judaizers who demanded circumcision. Perhaps Paul's earliest letter (c. AD 48-49).

- **1-2** **Personal:** Paul's gospel came by revelation
    - "If anyone is preaching to you a gospel contrary... let him be accursed" (1:8-9)
    - Paul's conversion and independence from Jerusalem (1:11-24)
    - Recognized by the Jerusalem apostles (2:1-10); confronts Peter at Antioch (2:11-14)
    - "A person is not justified by works of the law but through faith in Jesus Christ" (2:16); "I have been crucified with Christ" (2:20)
- **3-4** **Doctrinal:** justification by faith, not by the law
    - Abraham believed God (3:6); "the righteous shall live by faith" (3:11)
    - "Christ redeemed us from the curse of the law by becoming a curse for us" (3:13)
    - The law was added because of transgressions; the **law as guardian/schoolmaster** to bring us to Christ (3:24)
    - "There is neither Jew nor Greek... you are all one in Christ Jesus" (3:28)
    - Sons and heirs: adoption (4:4-7); allegory of Hagar and Sarah (4:21-31)
- **5-6** **Practical:** life in the freedom of the Spirit
    - "For freedom Christ has set us free" (5:1); do not use freedom for the flesh (5:13)
    - Walk by the Spirit; works of the flesh vs. the **fruit of the Spirit** (5:22-23)
    - Restore a brother gently; bear one another's burdens (6:1-2); sowing and reaping (6:7-8)
    - Boast only in the cross (6:14)

## Ephesians (6)

*God's Eternal Plan: One New People in Christ*

A Prison Epistle (Rome, c. AD 60-62). Memory aid: **sit** (1-3), **walk** (4-5), **stand** (6).

- **1-3** **Doctrine:** our position in Christ
    - **1:3-14** Every spiritual blessing: chosen by the Father, redeemed by the Son, sealed by the Spirit
    - **1:15-23** Prayer for enlightenment; Christ head over all things for the church
    - **2:1-10** Dead in sin, made alive: "by grace you have been saved through faith... not a result of works" (2:8-10)
    - **2:11-22** Jew and Gentile one new man; the dividing wall broken; a holy temple
    - **3** The mystery revealed; prayer to know the love of Christ (3:14-21)
- **4-6:9** **Duty:** our walk in Christ
    - **4:1-16** Unity in the body; gifts of Christ (apostles, prophets, evangelists, pastor-teachers) to equip the saints
    - **4:17-32** Put off the old self, put on the new
    - **5:1-21** Walk in love, light, and wisdom; "be filled with the Spirit" (5:18)
    - **5:22-6:9** Household code: wives and husbands (Christ and the church), children and fathers, slaves and masters
- **6:10-20** **Warfare:** the **armor of God**

## Philippians (4)

*Joy in Christ and Partnership in the Gospel*

A Prison Epistle, a thank-you letter to Paul's beloved Macedonian church.

- **1** Paul's circumstances: "he who began a good work in you will bring it to completion" (1:6); imprisonment advances the gospel; "to live is Christ, and to die is gain" (1:21)
- **2** The mind of Christ: humility and unity; the Christ hymn (2:5-11: *kenosis* and exaltation); "work out your own salvation... for it is God who works in you" (2:12-13); Timothy and Epaphroditus
- **3** Knowing Christ: beware of the Judaizers; Paul's credentials counted as loss; "righteousness from God that depends on faith" (3:9); **pressing toward the goal** (3:12-14); citizenship in heaven (3:20)
- **4** The peace of God: Euodia and Syntyche; "Rejoice in the Lord always"; "do not be anxious about anything" (4:6-7); contentment ("I can do all things through him who strengthens me," 4:13); thanks for their gift

## Colossians (4)

*The Supremacy and Sufficiency of Christ*

A Prison Epistle, written against a syncretistic "philosophy" (legalism, mysticism, asceticism, angel worship).

- **1-2** **Doctrine:** Christ is preeminent and sufficient
    - **1:15-20** Christ the image of God, firstborn over creation, creator, head of the church, reconciler of all things
    - **2:8-15** "In him the whole fullness of deity dwells bodily" (2:9); circumcision of Christ and baptism (2:11-12); the record of debt nailed to the cross
    - **2:16-23** Warnings against legalism, mysticism, and asceticism
- **3-4** **Duty:** life in Christ
    - **3:1-17** Set your minds on things above; put off and put on; "let the word of Christ dwell in you richly" (3:16)
    - **3:18-4:1** Household code
    - **4:2-18** Prayer, wise conduct, greetings (Tychicus, Onesimus, Epaphras, Luke, Mark)

## Summaries

### 1 Thessalonians (5)

*Encouragement and Hope in the Lord's Return.* Written from Corinth shortly after Paul's hurried departure from Thessalonica (Acts 17).

- **1-3** Thanksgiving for their faith; Paul's conduct among them; Timothy's good report
- **4:1-12** Holiness ("this is the will of God, your sanctification") and quiet work
- **4:13-18** **Not grieving as those who have no hope**: the dead in Christ will rise first; we will be caught up together
- **5** The day of the Lord comes like a thief; "pray without ceasing"

### 2 Thessalonians (3)

*Correcting Misunderstandings about the End Times.*

- **1** Encouragement in persecution; judgment at Christ's revelation
- **2** The day of the Lord has not come: first the rebellion and the **man of lawlessness** (2:1-12)
- **3** Warning against idleness: "if anyone is not willing to work, let him not eat" (3:10); discipline of the disorderly

### 1 Timothy (6)

*Leadership and Order in the Local Church.* To Timothy, Paul's delegate in Ephesus: "how one ought to behave in the household of God" (3:15).

- **1** Guard sound doctrine; "Christ Jesus came into the world to save sinners, of whom I am the foremost" (1:15)
- **2** Prayer for all, including rulers; one Mediator (2:5); women in worship (2:8-15)
- **3** **Qualifications** of overseers and deacons
- **4-6** Godly ministry ("let no one despise you for your youth," 4:12); widows; elders ("double honor"; discipline, 5:17-22); "the love of money is a root of all kinds of evils" (6:10); "fight the good fight of the faith" (6:12)

### 2 Timothy (4)

*Paul's Final Charge: Guard the Gospel and Preach the Word.* Written from prison, facing death.

- **1** Fan into flame the gift; "God gave us a spirit not of fear but of power and love and self-control" (1:7); guard the good deposit
- **2** A good soldier, athlete, farmer; "rightly handling the word of truth" (2:15)
- **3** Godlessness in the last days; "All Scripture is breathed out by God..." (**3:16-17**)
- **4** "Preach the word" (4:2); "**I have fought the good fight**, I have finished the race, I have kept the faith" (4:7)

### Titus (3)

*Appointing Elders and Devotion to Good Works in Crete.*

- **1** Appoint elders in every town: qualifications (1:5-9); silence false teachers
- **2** Teach sound doctrine to each group (older men and women, younger men, slaves); "the grace of God has appeared... training us" (2:11-14)
- **3** Be submissive to rulers; saved "not because of works... but according to his own mercy, by the **washing of regeneration** and renewal of the Holy Spirit" (**3:5**); warn a divisive person twice (3:10)

### Philemon (1)

*A plea for mercy and brotherhood for a runaway slave.* Paul sends Onesimus ("useful") back to his master Philemon in Colossae "no longer as a bondservant but... as a beloved brother" (v. 16), offering to pay any debt himself ("charge that to my account," v. 18): a picture of imputation and reconciliation.
