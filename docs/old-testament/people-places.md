# OT People & Places

<!-- cols:1 continuous -->

Chapell: *"Discuss briefly the life and significance of the following. Include book(s) and chapter(s)."* Names marked ★ are on Chapell's list; the rest are common follow-ups.

## Key People

| Person | Where | Significance |
|---|---|---|
| **Adam** ★ | Gen. 1-5; Rom. 5:12-21; 1 Cor. 15:22, 45 | First man, made in God's image; federal head under the covenant of works. His sin brought guilt and death to all; a type of Christ, the "last Adam." |
| **Noah** | Gen. 6-9 | Found favor; built the ark; the Flood; Noahic covenant (rainbow). |
| **Abraham** ★ | Gen. 12-25; Rom. 4; Gal. 3; Heb. 11 | Called from Ur; the Abrahamic covenant (12, 15, 17); justified by faith (15:6); offered Isaac (22). Father of the faithful. |
| **Melchizedek** | Gen. 14; Ps. 110:4; Heb. 7 | Priest-king of Salem who blessed Abram; a type of Christ's eternal priesthood. |
| **Isaac** | Gen. 21-28 | Son of promise; nearly sacrificed on Moriah; father of Jacob and Esau. |
| **Jacob (Israel)** | Gen. 25-50 | Took Esau's blessing; Bethel ladder (28); wrestled with God and renamed Israel (32); father of the twelve tribes. |
| **Joseph** ★ | Gen. 37-50 | Sold by his brothers; rose to rule Egypt; preserved Israel in famine. Shows God's providence ("God meant it for good," 50:20); a type of Christ. |
| **Moses** ★ | Exodus - Deuteronomy | Deliverer and mediator of the Sinai covenant; led the Exodus; received the law; the prophet whom a greater Prophet would fulfill (Deut. 18:15; Heb. 3). Died on Nebo. |
| **Aaron** | Ex. 4-32; Lev. 8-10; Num. 17 | Moses' brother; first high priest; made the golden calf. |
| **Joshua** ★ | Ex. 17; Num. 13-14; Joshua | Moses' assistant; faithful spy; led the conquest and division of Canaan; covenant renewal at Shechem (Josh. 24). Same name as "Jesus." |
| **Rahab** | Josh. 2, 6; Heb. 11:31; Matt. 1:5 | Canaanite prostitute who hid the spies; saved by faith; in Jesus' genealogy. |
| **Deborah** ★ | Judg. 4-5 | Prophetess and judge; with Barak defeated Sisera (killed by Jael); the Song of Deborah. |
| **Gideon** ★ | Judg. 6-8 | Judge; the fleece; 300 men defeat Midian, showing God's power in weakness; later made an ephod that became an idol. |
| **Samson** | Judg. 13-16 | Nazirite judge; great strength but weak character; died defeating the Philistines. |
| **Ruth** ★ | Ruth 1-4; Matt. 1:5 | Moabite widow, loyal to Naomi and Israel's God; married Boaz the kinsman-redeemer; great-grandmother of David. Gentile inclusion; *hesed*. |
| **Samuel** ★ | 1 Sam. 1-25 | Hannah's son; last judge and first of the great prophets; anointed Saul and David; "to obey is better than sacrifice" (15:22). |
| **Saul** | 1 Sam. 9-31 | Israel's first king; rejected for disobedience (13, 15); died on Mt. Gilboa. |
| **David** ★ | 1 Sam. 16 - 1 Kings 2; 1 Chron. 11-29; Psalms | Shepherd; anointed (1 Sam. 16); killed Goliath (17); king (c. 1010-970); took Jerusalem; Davidic covenant (2 Sam. 7); sinned with Bathsheba and repented (2 Sam. 11-12; Ps. 51). "Man after God's own heart"; ancestor and type of the Messiah. |
| **Solomon** ★ | 1 Kings 1-11; 2 Chron. 1-9 | David's son; asked for wisdom (1 Kings 3); built and dedicated the temple (6-8); many foreign wives led to idolatry (11). Proverbs, Ecclesiastes, Song. |
| **Rehoboam** ★ | 1 Kings 12, 14; 2 Chron. 10-12 | Solomon's son; harsh answer to the people caused the kingdom to divide (931/930 BC); first king of Judah. |
| **Jeroboam son of Nebat** ★ | 1 Kings 11-14 | First king of the northern kingdom; set up golden calves at Dan and Bethel. "The sins of Jeroboam son of Nebat" became the standard by which later northern kings were condemned. |
| **Ahab and Jezebel** | 1 Kings 16-22 | Wicked king of Israel and his Phoenician queen; promoted Baal worship; opposed by Elijah; Naboth's vineyard (21). |
| **Elijah** ★ | 1 Kings 17 - 2 Kings 2; Mal. 4:5; Matt. 17 | Prophet to Israel; drought; Mt. Carmel vs. the prophets of Baal (1 Kings 18); "still small voice" at Horeb (19); taken up in a whirlwind. John the Baptist came "in the spirit of Elijah"; appeared at the Transfiguration. |
| **Elisha** ★ | 1 Kings 19 - 2 Kings 13 | Elijah's successor with a "double portion"; many miracles (Naaman healed, 2 Kings 5; Shunammite's son raised, 4). |
| **Hezekiah** ★ | 2 Kings 18-20; 2 Chron. 29-32; Isa. 36-39 | Godly king of Judah (715-686); reforms; Jerusalem delivered from Sennacherib (701); healed with 15 years added; foolishly showed his treasures to Babylonian envoys. |
| **Manasseh** | 2 Kings 21; 2 Chron. 33 | Hezekiah's son; Judah's most wicked king; repented in captivity (Chronicles). |
| **Josiah** ★ | 2 Kings 22-23; 2 Chron. 34-35 | Boy king (640-609); the Book of the Law found (622); sweeping reforms; great Passover; killed by Pharaoh Neco at Megiddo. |
| **Isaiah** | Isa. 1-66; 2 Kings 19-20 | Prophet in Jerusalem (c. 740-681); vision of the Holy One (6); Immanuel; the suffering Servant. |
| **Jeremiah** ★ | Jeremiah; Lamentations | The "weeping prophet" (627-586) to Judah's last kings; warned of the Babylonian exile; persecuted; promised the new covenant (31:31-34). |
| **Nebuchadnezzar** | 2 Kings 24-25; Dan. 1-4 | King of Babylon; destroyed Jerusalem (586); humbled by God (Dan. 4). |
| **Ezekiel** ★ | Ezekiel | Priest-prophet among the exiles in Babylon (593-571); visions of God's glory leaving and returning; watchman (3, 33); dry bones (37); new temple. |
| **Daniel** ★ | Daniel | Taken to Babylon (605); faithful in the court; interpreted dreams; lions' den (6); apocalyptic visions of the kingdoms and the Son of Man (7); seventy weeks (9). |
| **Cyrus** | 2 Chron. 36; Ezra 1; Isa. 44:28-45:1 | Persian king whose decree (538) let the Jews return; named by Isaiah as God's "anointed." |
| **Zerubbabel** | Ezra 3-6; Hag.; Zech. 4 | Davidic governor; led the first return and rebuilt the temple (516). |
| **Esther and Mordecai** | Esther | Jewish queen of Xerxes and her cousin; saved the Jews from Haman's plot; Purim. |
| **Ezra** | Ezra 7-10; Neh. 8 | Priest and scribe; led the second return (458); taught the law; reformed mixed marriages. |
| **Nehemiah** ★ | Nehemiah | Cupbearer to Artaxerxes; governor who rebuilt Jerusalem's walls in 52 days (445/444) despite opposition; with Ezra led covenant renewal; model of prayerful leadership. |
| **Malachi** ★ | Malachi | Last OT prophet (c. 430); rebuked the priests and people for formalism, mixed marriage, divorce, and withheld tithes; promised the messenger and "Elijah" (3:1; 4:5), fulfilled in John the Baptist. |

## Key Places

| Place | Where | Significance |
|---|---|---|
| **Eden** | Gen. 2-3 | Garden sanctuary; the Fall |
| **Ararat** | Gen. 8:4 | Where the ark came to rest |
| **Babel / Babylon** | Gen. 11; 2 Kings 25; Daniel | Tower of Babel; later the empire that exiled Judah |
| **Ur of the Chaldeans** | Gen. 11:31 | Abraham's birthplace (Mesopotamia) |
| **Haran** | Gen. 11-12; 27-29 | Where Abraham's family settled; Jacob's refuge with Laban |
| **Shechem** | Gen. 12:6; Josh. 8, 24; John 4 | First stop in Canaan; covenant renewals between Mts. Ebal and Gerizim; later Samaritan Sychar |
| **Bethel** | Gen. 28; 1 Kings 12 | "House of God": Jacob's ladder; later Jeroboam's calf shrine |
| **Hebron (Mamre, Machpelah)** | Gen. 13, 18, 23 | Abraham's home and the patriarchs' burial cave; David's first capital |
| **Beersheba** | Gen. 21, 26 | Southern limit ("from Dan to Beersheba") |
| **Mt. Moriah** | Gen. 22; 2 Chron. 3:1 | Isaac offered; site of Solomon's temple |
| **Sodom and Gomorrah** | Gen. 18-19 | Destroyed for wickedness; Lot rescued |
| **Goshen (Egypt)** | Gen. 45-47; Ex. 8-9 | Israel's home in Egypt |
| **Mt. Sinai / Horeb** | Ex. 3, 19-40; 1 Kings 19 | Burning bush; giving of the law; Elijah's encounter with God |
| **Kadesh-barnea** | Num. 13-14, 20 | Spies sent; rebellion; 40 years of wandering |
| **Plains of Moab / Mt. Nebo** | Num. 22-36; Deut. 34 | Balaam; Moses' sermons (Deuteronomy); Moses' death |
| **Jordan River** | Josh. 3-4; 2 Kings 2, 5; Matt. 3 | Crossed into Canaan; Elijah taken up; Naaman healed; Jesus baptized |
| **Jericho** | Josh. 2, 6 | First city conquered |
| **Gilgal** | Josh. 4-5; 1 Sam. 11, 15 | First camp in Canaan; Saul made king and later rejected |
| **Shiloh** | Josh. 18; 1 Sam. 1-4 | Tabernacle's home; Samuel's boyhood; the ark captured |
| **Bethlehem** | Ruth; 1 Sam. 16; Mic. 5:2 | Home of Ruth and Boaz; David's town; birthplace of the Messiah |
| **Jerusalem / Zion** | 2 Sam. 5; 1 Kings 8; Ps. 48 | David's capital; the temple; destroyed 586; rebuilt |
| **Samaria** | 1 Kings 16; 2 Kings 17 | Capital of the northern kingdom; fell 722 |
| **Dan** | Judg. 18; 1 Kings 12 | Northern limit; Jeroboam's second calf shrine |
| **Mt. Carmel** | 1 Kings 18 | Elijah vs. the prophets of Baal |
| **Nineveh** | Jonah; Nahum | Assyrian capital; repented at Jonah's preaching; destroyed 612 |
| **Susa (Shushan)** | Esther; Neh. 1 | Persian royal city; Esther and Nehemiah |
