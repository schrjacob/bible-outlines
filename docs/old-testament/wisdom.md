# Poetry & Wisdom

!!! tip "Exam level: book contents (summary)"
    Chapell lists Job through Song of Songs under **Book Contents**. Know the one-line summary of each book. For Psalms, know the **five books**, the main **genres**, and the **messianic psalms** (2, 22, 110), which Chapell names as a key passage.

## Job (42)

*Suffering and the Wisdom and Sovereignty of God*

- **1-2** Prologue in heaven: Satan's challenge; Job's losses; Job does not curse God
- **3-37** Dialogues: Job and his "miserable comforters" (Eliphaz, Bildad, Zophar), then Elihu
    - **19:25** "I know that my Redeemer lives"
    - **28** Wisdom hymn: wisdom is found in the fear of the Lord
- **38-41** God answers from the whirlwind: not an explanation but a display of his majesty and wisdom in creation
- **42** Job's repentance and restoration

### Key Themes

- Who is wise? (God)
- The retributive principle: is suffering always punishment for sin?
- Disinterested righteousness: *"Does Job fear God for no reason?"* (1:9)
- Miserable comforters (Eliphaz, Bildad, Zophar, and Elihu)

## Psalms (150)

*Songs, Prayers, and Praises to God*

| Book | Psalms | Primary theme/focus |
|---|---|---|
| I | 1-41 | Mainly Davidic; God's presence and individual lament |
| II | 42-72 | Uses *Elohim* (God) rather than *Yahweh* (LORD); ends with Solomon's reign |
| III | 73-89 | Crisis of the Davidic covenant; many psalms of Asaph and the sons of Korah |
| IV | 90-106 | Reflects on the exile; Moses (90) and the kingship of God |
| V | 107-150 | Thanksgiving and return; the Hallel and Songs of Ascent |

Each book ends with a doxology (41:13; 72:18-19; 89:52; 106:48; 150).

### Genres and Types of Psalms

- **Messianic:** direct prophecy (110), typological (e.g., David), first-person experience. Know especially **2, 22, 110**.
    - **2** "The LORD said to me, 'You are my Son; today I have begotten you'" (7)
    - **16** "You will not abandon my soul to Sheol, or let your holy one see corruption" (10)
    - **22** "My God, my God, why have you forsaken me?" (1); "they have pierced my hands and feet" (16)
    - **34** "He keeps all his bones; not one of them is broken" (20)
    - **40** "In sacrifice and offering you have not delighted" (6); "in the scroll of the book it is written of me" (7)
    - **41** "Even my close friend in whom I trusted, who ate my bread, has lifted his heel against me" (9)
    - **110** "The LORD says to my Lord, 'Sit at my right hand...'" (1); Melchizedek (4)
    - **118** "The stone that the builders rejected has become the cornerstone" (22)
- **Lament:** cries to God for help in distress (13, 22, 74, 77, 88)
- **Praise:** declarations of God's character, attributes, and works (103, 107, 117, 145)
- **Wisdom:** reflections on the law and the righteous life (1, 19, 37, 73, 119)
- **Imprecatory:** an appeal to divine justice, not personal vengeance (35, 59, 69, 109, 137)
- **Penitential:** psalms of repentance (6, 32, 38, 51, 102, 130, 143)
- **Royal:** psalms for the (messianic) king (2, 18, 20, 21, 45, 72, 89, 110, 132)
- **Sons of Korah:** intense longing for God and temple worship (42-49, 84, 85, 87, 88)
- **Hallel:** Passover songs, remembering the Exodus and Red Sea (113-118)
- **Great Hallel:** 136
- **Songs of Ascent:** pilgrim songs (120-134)

## Proverbs (31)

*Wisdom for God's People: the fear of the LORD is the beginning of wisdom*

- **1-9** A father's invitation to wisdom
    - **1:7** "The fear of the LORD is the beginning of knowledge; fools despise wisdom and instruction."
    - **3:5-6** "Trust in the LORD with all your heart, and do not lean on your own understanding. In all your ways acknowledge him, and he will make straight your paths."
    - **5-7** Warnings against the adulteress
    - **8** Wisdom's role in creation (Christological)
    - **9** Lady Wisdom and the woman Folly
- **10-29** Proverbs of Solomon (and others): short sayings for everyday life
- **30-31** Sayings of Agur and Lemuel
    - **31** The "excellent wife"

## Ecclesiastes (12)

*Wisdom in the Fear of God amid the Vanities of Life under the Sun*

### Key Themes

- ***Hevel* (vanity/vapor/enigma):** life is fleeting and often incomprehensible, "a striving after wind"
- **Life "under the sun":** human existence viewed from a this-worldly perspective; apart from God, everything is meaningless
- **The gift of God:** find joy in simple gifts (eating, drinking, and working) as from the hand of God

### Key Passages

- **3:1-8** A time for everything
- **12:1-8** Remember your Creator in the days of your youth
- **12:13-14** The "end of the matter": fear God and keep his commandments

## Song of Songs (8)

*Love as Strong as Death: a picture of the LORD's covenant love for his people*

- **1-2** The lovers yearn for each other
- **3-5** The Shepherdess dreams
- **6-7** The lovers yearn for each other again
- **8** The lovers join in marriage ("love is strong as death," 8:6)

### Key Themes

- **The beauty of marriage:** romantic love is a good gift from God
- **Covenant faithfulness:** the intensity and exclusivity of the couple's love reflects the "sealed" nature of the covenant
- **Redemptive significance:** a picture of the love between Christ and his church (Eph. 5)
