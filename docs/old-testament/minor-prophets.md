# Minor Prophets (The Twelve)

!!! tip "Exam level: book contents (summary)"
    Chapell lists all twelve under **Book Contents**: know a one-line summary, roughly **when** each prophet ministered, and **to whom**. Use the key verses as hooks. A table that groups them by period is at the bottom of this page.

## Hosea (14)

*God loves and reclaims his faithless people; he never lets go.*

- **1-3** Gomer unfaithful; Hosea reclaims her in love
- **4-14** Israel unfaithful; God reclaims Israel in love

**Key verse:** "For I desire steadfast love and not sacrifice, the knowledge of God rather than burnt offerings" (**6:6**; quoted by Jesus in Matt. 9:13; 12:7). Also "Out of Egypt I called my son" (**11:1**; Matt. 2:15).

## Joel (3)

*The Day of the LORD: Plague, Penitence, and Promise*

- **1:1-2:11** A plague of locusts
- **2:12-17** A call to repentance: "rend your hearts and not your garments"
- **2:18-3:21** God's mercy; judgment of the nations
    - **2:18-27** The locusts removed
    - **2:28-32** "I will pour out my Spirit on all flesh" (fulfilled at Pentecost, Acts 2)
    - **2:32** "Everyone who calls on the name of the LORD shall be saved" (Rom. 10:13)
    - **3:1-16** The nations judged
    - **3:17-21** Judah's future glory

## Amos (9)

*The Universal Justice of God*

The "shepherd of Tekoa," a southerner sent to the northern kingdom (c. 760 BC).

- **1-2:5** Judgment on Israel's neighbors
- **2:6-6:14** Particular judgment on Israel
- **7-9** Five visions of judgment; restoration of "the booth of David" (9:11-12; Acts 15:16-17)

**Key verse:** "Let justice roll down like waters, and righteousness like an ever-flowing stream" (5:24).

## Obadiah (1)

*God will humble Edom, who rejoiced in his brother's distress.*

## Jonah (4)

*The Boundless Compassion of God*

- **A** 1:1-3 Jonah's commissioning and flight
    - **B** 1:4-16 Jonah and the pagan sailors
        - **C** 1:17-2:10 Jonah's grateful prayer
- **A'** 3:1-3a Jonah's recommissioning and compliance
    - **B'** 3:3b-10 Jonah and the pagan Ninevites
        - **C'** 4:1-4 Jonah's angry prayer
            - **D** 4:5-11 God's lesson about compassion

## Micah (7)

*Judgment and Forgiveness: God's "lawsuit" against his people*

- **1-2** Controversy with the capitals (Jerusalem and Samaria)
- **3-5** Controversy with the rulers (prophets, priests, princes)
    - **4** "Swords into plowshares"; "every man under his vine and under his fig tree"
    - **5:2** "But you, O Bethlehem Ephrathah..." (Matt. 2:6)
- **6-7** Controversy with the people
    - **6:8** "What does the LORD require of you but to do justice, and to love kindness, and to walk humbly with your God?"
    - **7:18-19** "Who is a God like you, pardoning iniquity?"

## Nahum (3)

*The LORD's vengeance and goodness revealed in Nineveh's overthrow (background to Rev. 17-18)*

- **1:1-8** A psalm of praise to the LORD
- **1:9-15** Coming judgment on Nineveh; deliverance for Judah
- **2-3** Focus on Nineveh: the coming judgment (Nineveh fell in 612 BC)

## Habakkuk (3)

*The righteous shall live by faith (2:4), not by sight or understanding.*

- **1:1** Superscription
- **1:2-4** Habakkuk's first complaint: why do the wicked go unpunished in Judah?
- **1:5-11** God's response: I am raising up the Babylonians
- **1:12-2:20** Habakkuk's second complaint, and God's answer: "the righteous shall live by his faith" (2:4; Rom. 1:17; Gal. 3:11; Heb. 10:38); five woes on Babylon
- **3** Habakkuk's prayer: "though the fig tree should not blossom... yet I will rejoice in the LORD" (3:17-18)

## Zephaniah (3)

*The Day of the LORD: judgment upon sinners; blessing for God's people.*

**Key verse:** "The LORD your God is in your midst... he will rejoice over you with gladness... he will exult over you with loud singing" (3:17).

## Haggai (2)

*An exhortation to rebuild the LORD's house (520 BC).*

- **1** An exhortation ("Is it a time for you yourselves to dwell in your paneled houses, while this house lies in ruins?") and an obedient response
- **2:1-9** The former and latter glory
- **2:10-19** Holiness and defilement; repentance and blessing
- **2:20-23** Zerubbabel the signet ring

## Zechariah (14)

*The Future Glory of Zion and of Its Shepherd King*

- **1-8** Oracles and visions about rebuilding the temple and spiritual renewal
    - **1:1-6** Introduction: "Return to me, and I will return to you"
    - **1:7-6:15** Eight night visions and a sign-act (crowning of Joshua the high priest)
    - **7-8** From fasts to feasts
- **9-14** Two oracles: the Messiah's rejection and ultimate reign
    - **9-11** First oracle: leaders and their people ("your king is coming to you... humble and mounted on a donkey," 9:9; thirty pieces of silver, 11:12-13)
    - **12-14** Second oracle: the people and their leaders ("they look on me, on him whom they have pierced," 12:10; "strike the shepherd," 13:7)

## Malachi (4)

*The LORD of hosts calls Israel to repent of their cynicism, compromise, and religious malaise (c. 430 BC).*

- **1:1** Heading
- **A** 1:2-5 "How have you loved us?" God's elective love vindicated in judgment ("Jacob I loved, but Esau I hated"; Rom. 9:13)
    - **B** 1:6-2:9 Begrudging offerings and corrupt priests condemned
        - **C** 2:10-16 Marriage to idolaters and divorce condemned ("I hate divorce")
        - **C'** 2:17-3:5 The LORD will come as witness against adultery and other moral offenses: "Behold, I send my messenger" (**3:1**)
    - **B'** 3:6-12 Israel's begrudging offerings (tithes) condemned
- **A'** 3:13-4:3 God distinguishes between the righteous and the wicked
- **4:4-6** Conclusion: "I will send you Elijah the prophet... and he will turn the hearts of fathers to their children" (fulfilled in John the Baptist; Matt. 11:14)

|---|---|---|---|
| **Assyrian period** | Jonah | Nineveh (from Israel) | c. 785-760 |
| | Amos | Israel (north) | c. 760 |
| | Hosea | Israel (north) | c. 755-715 |
| | Isaiah | Judah | c. 740-681 |
| | Micah | Judah (and Samaria) | c. 735-700 |

| | Zephaniah | Judah | c. 640-621 |
| | Jeremiah | Judah | c. 627-586 |
| | Habakkuk | Judah | c. 609-598 |
| | Daniel | Exiles in Babylon | 605-536 |
| | Ezekiel | Exiles in Babylon | 593-571 |
| | Obadiah | Edom (for Judah) | c. 586 |
| **Persian period** | Haggai | Returned remnant | 520 |
| | Zechariah | Returned remnant | 520-480 |
| | Malachi | Returned remnant | c. 430 |
| **Uncertain** | Joel | Judah | (early 9th c. or post-exilic) |

**Memory aid:** pre-exilic prophets warn (*repent!*); exilic prophets console (*God is still sovereign*); post-exilic prophets rebuild (*finish the temple, keep the covenant*).
