## Genesis (50)

*The Book of Beginnings*

- **1-2** Creation; dominion; marriage
- **3** Fall and curse
    - **3:15** Protoevangelium (first gospel promise)
- **4-11** Fallout of sin
    - **4** Cain and Abel
    - **5** Line of Seth
    - **6-9** Noah and the Flood; Noahic covenant (**9**)
    - **10** Table of Nations
    - **11** Tower of Babel
- **12-50** Patriarchal history
    - **12-25** Abraham
        - **12** Call and promise (land, seed, blessing)
        - **14** Melchizedek
        - **15** Abrahamic covenant ("believed... counted it to him as righteousness," 15:6)
        - **17** Sign of circumcision
        - **22** Isaac on Mt. Moriah
    - **24-26** Isaac, the son of promise
        - **24** Rebekah, a wife for Isaac
        - **26** Isaac and Abimelech
    - **27-36** Jacob, continuation of the covenant
        - **27** Stolen blessing
        - **28** Ladder to heaven; Bethel
        - **29** Leah and Rachel
        - **30** Jacob's children
        - **31** Flight from Laban
        - **32** Wrestling with God; renamed Israel
        - **33** Jacob meets Esau
        - **34** Dinah and Shechem, Levi and Simeon
    - **37-50** Joseph and his brothers: God's providence preserves the family and moves it to Egypt
        - **37** Dreams; sold into slavery
        - **38** Judah and Tamar
        - **39** Potiphar's wife
        - **40** Baker and cupbearer
        - **41** Pharaoh's dreams
        - **42-46** Joseph and his brothers
        - **47** Egypt consolidated; Jacob moves to Egypt
        - **48-50** Jacob's blessings (Judah's scepter, **49:10**); deaths of Jacob and Joseph ("you meant evil... God meant it for good," **50:20**)

### Key Themes

- Creator and creation
- Covenants of works and grace
- Man's fall into sin
- Human depravity
- Preservation of the seed
- Deceiving the "serpent"
- Sons of promise; the younger brother
- Providence of God

## Exodus (40)

*Israel Called and Consecrated*

- **1-18** Israel called out of Egypt
    - **1-6** Persecution of God's people
    - **3** Burning bush; YHWH
    - **7-12** Ten Plagues

- **19-40** Israel consecrated at Sinai
    - **19-24** Covenant established; law given
    - **25-31** Tabernacle and its furnishings
    - **32-34** Covenant broken; intercession; covenant renewed
    - **35-40** Tabernacle built

- **18** Jethro's advice
- **19** Sinai
- **20** Ten Commandments
- **24** Covenant ratified by blood
- **31** The Sabbath, a covenant sign; stone tablets given

## Leviticus (27)

*The LORD's Laws of Fellowship*

- **1-16** How sinners **attain** fellowship with God
    - **1-7** Oblation (offerings)
    - **8-10** Mediation (priesthood; Nadab and Abihu, **10**)
    - **11-15** Distinction (clean/unclean, common/holy)
    - **16** Expiation (Day of Atonement)
- **17-27** How sinners **maintain** fellowship with God
    - **17-22** Sanctification (food, drink, sex); "You shall be holy, for I the LORD your God am holy" (**19:2**); "love your neighbor as yourself" (**19:18**)
    - **23-25** Celebration (religious festivals; Sabbath year, Jubilee)
    - **26-27** Ratification (promises and threats)

### Types of Offerings (1-5)

- **Burnt/ascension (*olah*):** a *whole* offering. The LORD accepts with pleasure whoever comes into his presence by substitutionary atonement through the shedding of blood; also, the worshiper offers *himself*.
- **Grain/tribute:** God's people offer themselves and their best as a token of dedication and gratitude.
- **Peace:** God's people celebrate being at peace with God in a communal meal.
- **Sin/purification:** God forgives and cleanses our sins and uncleanness by sacrificial blood.
- **Guilt/trespass/reparation:** Forgiveness and atonement for obligations left undone or impure contacts left unpurified.

The first three are like food (ongoing); the last two like medicine (*pro re nata*).

## Numbers (36)

*Journey to the Promised Land; Lessons in Sin and Grace*

- **1-10** Preparation at Sinai
    - First census
    - Organization of the camp
    - Consecration of tabernacle and Levites
- **11-25** Grumbling, rebellion, and failure
    - Journey to Kadesh-barnea
    - Spies' report and rebellion; 40 years of wandering
- **26-36** Preparation for the new generation
    - Second census
    - Appointment of Joshua

### Key Texts

- **6** Nazirite vow (total consecration)
- **6:24-26** Aaronic blessing: "The LORD bless you and keep you; the LORD make his face to shine upon you and be gracious to you; the LORD lift up his countenance upon you and give you peace."
- **11** Quail and a plague
- **12** Miriam and Aaron oppose Moses
- **13** Spies in Canaan
- **14** Rebellion, intercession, judgment, defeat
- **16-17** Korah's rebellion; Aaron's rod buds (doctrine of priesthood)
- **20** Meribah: Moses strikes the rock and is barred from Canaan
- **21** Bronze serpent (type of Christ; John 3:14)
- **22-24** Balaam's oracles (God's sovereignty over the nations; "a star shall come out of Jacob," 24:17)

- **27** Joshua to succeed Moses

### Themes

- God's presence
- Need for a mediator
- Depravity: the "book of grumbling"
- Covenant faithfulness: God preserves a remnant

## Deuteronomy (34)

*The "Sermons of Moses": an ANE suzerainty treaty*

- **1-4** Preamble and historical prologue ("God has dealt graciously with you from Sinai to Moab")
- **5-26** Covenant stipulations
    - **5-11** Moral core
    - **12-26** Specific statutes (application to worship, leadership, and civil life)
- **27-30** Covenant sanctions: blessings and curses; obedience brings life, disobedience death and exile
- **31-34** Succession and final words: Moses commissions Joshua, sings his song, blesses the tribes, and dies on **Mount Nebo**

### Key Chapters

- **5** Ten Commandments repeated (rooted in redemption from slavery)
- **6** Shema; teach your children; the greatest commandment
- **13** False prophets
- **17** Law of the king (anticipating a monarchy)
- **18** A prophet like Moses
- **24** Divorce and remarriage (cf. Matt. 19)
- **27-28** Blessings and curses from Mt. Ebal and Mt. Gerizim
- **34** Death of Moses

### Key Verses

- **6:4-5** (The Shema): "Hear, O Israel: The LORD our God, the LORD is one. You shall love the LORD your God with all your heart and with all your soul and with all your might."
- **18:15** (Messianic promise): "The LORD your God will raise up for you a prophet like me from among you, from your brothers; it is to him you shall listen."
- **29:29** (The secret things): "The secret things belong to the LORD our God, but the things that are revealed belong to us and to our children forever..."
- **30:19** (The choice): "I have set before you life and death, blessing and curse. Therefore choose life, that you and your offspring may live."

### Theological Concepts

- **Circumcision of the heart:** "Circumcise therefore the foreskin of your heart, and be no longer stubborn" (**10:16**); "the LORD your God will circumcise your heart and the heart of your offspring, so that you will love the LORD your God..." (**30:6**)
- **Regulative principle of worship:** "Everything that I command you, you shall be careful to do. You shall not add to it or take from it" (**12:32**).
