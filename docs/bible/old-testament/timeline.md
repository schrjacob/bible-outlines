# OT Timeline & Covenants

<!-- cols:1 continuous -->

Chapell asks for *"a general outline of Old Testament history, including key dates"* and *"a general outline of Old Testament history from the perspective of the covenant, including key passages."* Dates follow the conservative chronology common in Reformed seminaries (e.g., an early Exodus, 1446 BC).

!!! tip "Anchor dates: memorize these first"
    **2000** Abraham · **1446** Exodus · **1000** David · **931** division · **722** fall of Samaria · **586** fall of Jerusalem · **538** return · **516** second temple · **c. 430** Malachi, the end of OT revelation

    A rough "every 500 years" frame: **2000** Abraham, **1500** Moses, **1000** David, **500** return from exile, **0** Christ.

## OT History through the Covenants

The Westminster Confession (ch. 7) frames all of Scripture by covenant: a **covenant of works** with Adam, and after the Fall a single **covenant of grace**, administered progressively through the historical covenants until it is fulfilled in Christ.

| Covenant | Key passages | Parties / mediator | Promise and content | Sign |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Redemption** (eternal) | Ps. 2:7-8; John 6:37-39; 17:4-6; Eph. 1:3-11 | Father and Son (and Spirit) | The Son will redeem the elect given him | (none) |
| **Works** (life) | Gen. 2:15-17; Hos. 6:7; Rom. 5:12-21 | God and Adam (as federal head) | Life for perfect obedience; death for disobedience | Tree of life |
| **Grace announced** | **Gen. 3:15** | God and fallen humanity | The seed of the woman will crush the serpent | Garments of skin (3:21) |
| **Noahic** (preservation) | Gen. 6:18; 8:20-9:17 | God, Noah, all creation | No more global flood; a stable world in which redemption can unfold | Rainbow |
| **Abrahamic** (promise) | **Gen. 12:1-3; 15; 17**; Gal. 3:6-29 | God and Abraham and his seed | Land, seed, blessing to the nations; "I will be your God" | Circumcision |
| **Mosaic** (law) | **Ex. 19-24**; Deut. | God and Israel, through Moses | Israel a "kingdom of priests"; the law as the rule of life for the redeemed | Sabbath (Ex. 31:13) |
| **Davidic** (kingdom) | **2 Sam. 7**; 1 Chron. 17; Ps. 89; 132 | God and David's house | An eternal throne, house, and kingdom; God as Father to David's son | (throne/temple) |
| **New** (fulfillment) | **Jer. 31:31-34**; Ezek. 36:26-27; Luke 22:20; Heb. 8-10 | God and his people, through Christ | Law written on the heart; full forgiveness; all will know the LORD; the Spirit given | Baptism and the Lord's Supper |

!!! note "How the covenant of grace relates to the others (Chapell, Theology E)"
    - **Abrahamic:** the clearest OT statement of the covenant of grace; justification by faith (Gen. 15:6; Rom. 4; Gal. 3).
    - **Mosaic:** an administration of the covenant of grace (Ex. 20:2: redemption comes *before* law). It also contains a typological "works" principle tied to life in the land.
    - **Noahic:** a common-grace covenant that preserves the world as the stage for redemption.
    - **Old vs. new:** one covenant of grace under different administrations (WCF 7.5-6). In the OT it is promised in types and shadows; in the NT it is fulfilled in Christ, with greater fullness, clarity, and reach to all nations.

### Covenant Storyline in One Breath

Creation and the covenant of works (Gen. 1-2), then the **Fall** (Gen. 3) and the promise of grace (3:15). The world is preserved through **Noah** (Gen. 9); the promise is narrowed to **Abraham's** seed (Gen. 12, 15, 17), redeemed and given the law through **Moses** (Ex. 19-24), focused on **David's** royal son (2 Sam. 7), broken by Israel and judged in **exile** (722, 586), and renewed in hope of a **new covenant** (Jer. 31). It is fulfilled in **Christ**, the true Seed, Prophet, Priest, and King (Luke 22:20; Heb. 8).
