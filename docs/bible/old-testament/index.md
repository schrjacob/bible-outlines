# Old Testament

<!-- cols:2 continuous -->

**39 books.** Chapell asks: *"Name the general divisions of the Old Testament and the books in each."* Know the **English (Christian) order** cold. The **Hebrew order** (Law, Prophets, Writings, or *Tanakh*) is good to know because Jesus refers to it ("the Law of Moses and the Prophets and the Psalms," Luke 24:44).

## English Divisions

### The Pentateuch / Law (5)

1. **Genesis:** the beginning of the world and of the chosen family
2. **Exodus:** deliverance from Egypt and the giving of the law
3. **Leviticus:** instructions for holy living and sacrificial worship
4. **Numbers:** the wilderness wandering and the census of Israel
5. **Deuteronomy:** a second telling of the law to a new generation

### History (12)

6. **Joshua:** the conquest and settlement of the Promised Land
7. **Judges:** a cycle of rebellion, oppression, and temporary deliverers
8. **Ruth:** loyalty and redemption in a dark time
9. **1 Samuel:** the transition from judges to the first king, Saul
10. **2 Samuel:** the reign of King David and his covenant
11. **1 Kings:** the reign of Solomon and the division of the kingdom
12. **2 Kings:** the decline and fall of the divided kingdoms
13. **1 Chronicles:** a priestly perspective on David's spiritual legacy
14. **2 Chronicles:** the history of Judah's kings and the temple
15. **Ezra:** the return from exile and rebuilding of the temple
16. **Nehemiah:** the rebuilding of Jerusalem's walls and spiritual reform
17. **Esther:** God's silent providence saving his people in Persia

### Poetry & Wisdom (5)

18. **Job:** suffering and the wisdom and sovereignty of God
19. **Psalms:** songs, prayers, and praises to God
20. **Proverbs:** practical wisdom for godly and skillful living
21. **Ecclesiastes:** the search for meaning in a "vanity"-filled world
22. **Song of Songs:** a picture of the LORD's covenant love for his people

### Major Prophets (5)

23. **Isaiah:** judgment and the coming Messiah and Servant
24. **Jeremiah:** the "weeping prophet" warns of exile and promises a new covenant
25. **Lamentations:** a funeral dirge for the fallen city of Jerusalem
26. **Ezekiel:** God's glory leaving and returning to the temple
27. **Daniel:** God's sovereignty over pagan empires and the future

### Minor Prophets (12)

28. **Hosea:** God's faithful love for unfaithful Israel
29. **Joel:** the Day of the LORD and the promise of the Spirit
30. **Amos:** a call for justice and true worship
31. **Obadiah:** judgment against Edom
32. **Jonah:** a reluctant prophet and God's mercy on Nineveh
33. **Micah:** justice, mercy, and humility; the ruler from Bethlehem
34. **Nahum:** the certain destruction of Assyria (Nineveh)
35. **Habakkuk:** a dialogue with God about injustice; living by faith
36. **Zephaniah:** the coming judgment and the restoration of a remnant
37. **Haggai:** a challenge to finish rebuilding the LORD's house
38. **Zechariah:** messianic visions and encouragement for the returning exiles
39. **Malachi:** a final warning against spiritual apathy; the coming messenger

## Hebrew Divisions (Tanakh)

### The Law (*Torah*)

- Genesis, Exodus, Leviticus, Numbers, Deuteronomy

### The Former Prophets (*Nevi'im*)

- Joshua
- Judges
- 1 & 2 Samuel
- 1 & 2 Kings

### The Latter Prophets

- Isaiah
- Jeremiah
- Ezekiel
- The Twelve (Hosea through Malachi)

### The Writings (*Ketuvim*)

- Psalms, Proverbs, Job
- The five scrolls (*Megilloth*): Song of Songs, Ruth, Lamentations, Ecclesiastes, Esther
- Daniel
- Ezra-Nehemiah
- 1 & 2 Chronicles

The Hebrew canon contains the same books as the Protestant OT, counted as 24 (the Twelve as one book; Samuel, Kings, Chronicles, and Ezra-Nehemiah as one each). It ends with Chronicles, so Jesus' "from the blood of Abel to the blood of Zechariah" (Matt. 23:35; 2 Chron. 24:20-21) spans the whole canon.

### The Latter Prophets in Rough Chronological Order

- Jonah, Amos, Hosea (to Israel, the north)
- Micah (Israel and Judah), Isaiah
- Nahum, Zephaniah, Habakkuk, Jeremiah, Obadiah
- Ezekiel (in exile)
- Haggai, Zechariah, Malachi (after the return)
- Joel (date uncertain)
