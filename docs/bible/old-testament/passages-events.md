# OT Key Passages & Events

<!-- cols:1 continuous -->

Chapell, sections D and E: *"Locate the following passages by book and chapter"* and *"Locate these events by book and chapter (and date where asterisked)."* Chapell's items are marked ★; the others are frequent follow-ups.

## Key Passages

| Passage | Location | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| **Passover** ★ | **Ex. 12** | Lamb's blood on the doorposts; fulfilled in Christ our Passover (1 Cor. 5:7) |
| **Ten Commandments** ★ | **Ex. 20:1-17; Deut. 5:6-21** | Preface: "I am the LORD your God, who brought you out of... slavery" |
| **Day of Atonement** ★ | **Lev. 16** (also 23:26-32) | Two goats; high priest enters the Most Holy Place once a year; Heb. 9 |
| **Shema and reiteration of the law** ★ | **Deut. 6:4-9** (law restated **Deut. 5**) | "Hear, O Israel... you shall love the LORD your God with all your heart" |
| **Watchman** ★ | **Ezek. 3:16-21; 33:1-9** | The prophet's responsibility to warn the wicked |
| **New covenant** ★ | **Jer. 31:31-34** | Law on the heart; quoted in Heb. 8:8-12; 10:16-17 |
| **"I will pour out my Spirit"** ★ | **Joel 2:28-32** | Fulfilled at Pentecost (Acts 2:16-21) |
| **"The just shall live by faith"** ★ | **Hab. 2:4** | Rom. 1:17; Gal. 3:11; Heb. 10:38 |
| **"I am going to send my messenger"** ★ | **Mal. 3:1** (and 4:5-6) | John the Baptist (Matt. 11:10; Mark 1:2) |
| **Messianic psalms** ★ | **Pss. 2, 22, 110** (also 16, 45, 72, 118) | Ps. 110:1 is the OT verse most quoted in the NT |
| Protoevangelium | Gen. 3:15 | The seed of the woman will crush the serpent's head |
| Call of and covenant with Abraham | Gen. 12:1-3; 15; 17 | Gen. 15:6, "he believed the LORD, and he counted it to him as righteousness" |
| Binding of Isaac | Gen. 22 | "God will provide for himself the lamb" |
| Burning bush; divine name | Ex. 3 | "I AM WHO I AM" (3:14) |
| God's character revealed | Ex. 34:6-7 | "merciful and gracious, slow to anger, abounding in steadfast love" |
| Aaronic blessing | Num. 6:24-26 | "The LORD bless you and keep you" |
| Prophet like Moses | Deut. 18:15-22 | Acts 3:22; 7:37 |
| Davidic covenant | 2 Sam. 7:12-16 | "I will establish the throne of his kingdom forever" |
| David's repentance | Ps. 51 | After Bathsheba (2 Sam. 11-12) |
| Isaiah's vision | Isa. 6 | "Holy, holy, holy"; "Here I am! Send me." |
| Immanuel | Isa. 7:14 | Matt. 1:23 |
| Suffering Servant | Isa. 52:13-53:12 | Acts 8:32-35; 1 Pet. 2:22-25 |
| New heart, new spirit | Ezek. 36:25-27 | Regeneration; John 3:5 |
| Valley of dry bones | Ezek. 37 | Resurrection and restoration |
| Son of Man | Dan. 7:13-14 | Jesus' favorite self-designation |
| Ruler from Bethlehem | Mic. 5:2 | Matt. 2:6 |
| "What does the LORD require?" | Mic. 6:8 | Do justice, love kindness, walk humbly |
| King on a donkey | Zech. 9:9 | Triumphal entry (Matt. 21:5) |
