### The Journey to Rome (Acts 27-28)

From Caesarea under guard (the centurion Julius) > Myra > Fair Havens on Crete > storm and **shipwreck on Malta** (Paul bitten by a viper, unharmed) > Puteoli > **Rome**: two years under house arrest, preaching the kingdom "without hindrance."

### Why the Order Matters

- The Jerusalem Council (Acts 15) came between the first and second journeys and settled the question Galatians addresses: Gentiles are not required to be circumcised.
- Churches Paul planted on the second journey (Philippi, Thessalonica, Corinth) received his letters on the third journey and later.
- Ephesus (third journey, about 3 years) became the hub for the province of Asia, the setting of 1 Timothy and Revelation 2-3.
