# The Gospels

<!-- cols:1 continuous -->

!!! info "Exam level: full outlines"
    Chapell expects **outlines of all four Gospels** and asks: *"What are the distinctive features of each of the four gospels?"* Matthew and John get the most detail here: Matthew because it holds the most "locate this passage" items, John because it is the most distinctive.

## Distinctives of the Four Gospels

| | Matthew | Mark | Luke | John |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Jesus as** | Promised King, Messiah, new Moses | Suffering Servant, Son of God in action | Son of Man, Savior of all | Eternal Word, Son of God |
| **Audience** | Jews / Jewish Christians | Romans / Gentiles | Theophilus; Gentiles | Everyone: "that you may believe" |
| **Key verse** | 1:1; 5:17; 28:18-20 | 10:45 | 19:10 | 20:30-31 |
| **Genealogy** | From Abraham (1:1-17) | None (a servant needs no pedigree) | Back to Adam (3:23-38) | Eternal pre-existence (1:1-18) |
| **Marks** | OT fulfillment formulas ("to fulfill what was spoken"); **five discourses**; "kingdom of heaven"; only Gospel to use "church" (16, 18) | Shortest; fast-paced ("immediately"); vivid detail from Peter; "messianic secret"; a third devoted to Passion Week | "Orderly account" (1:1-4); poor, women, outcasts, Gentiles; prayer; Holy Spirit; joy; songs (Magnificat); travel narrative; volume 1 of Luke-Acts | 90% unique; 7 signs; 7 "I am" sayings; long discourses; Judean ministry; Upper Room Discourse (13-17) |

## Matthew (28)

*Jesus, the Christ: the promised King and the Great Prophet*

Built around **five discourses**, each ending "when Jesus had finished these sayings" (7:28; 11:1; 13:53; 19:1; 26:1), perhaps echoing the five books of Moses: **5-7** Sermon on the Mount; **10** mission; **13** parables; **18** church life; **24-25** Olivet.

- **1-2** Prologue
    - **1** Genealogy and birth ("Immanuel," 1:23)
    - **2** Magi; flight to Egypt; Bethlehem massacre; return to Nazareth
- **3-7** The gospel of the kingdom
    - **3** John the Baptist; baptism of Jesus
    - **4** Temptation and early Galilean ministry
    - **5-7** **Sermon on the Mount** (see below)
- **8-10** The kingdom extended under Jesus' authority
    - **8:1-9:34** Kingdom miracles (power over disease, creation, evil spirits, death)
    - **10** **The Twelve sent to preach** (mission, suffering, courage, cross-bearing, rewards)
- **11-13** Teaching and preaching the kingdom; rising opposition
    - **11:2-19** The roles of Jesus and John the Baptist
    - **11:20-30** Woe to unrepentant cities; rest for the weary ("Take my yoke," 11:28-30)
    - **12** Miracles and confrontation with the Pharisees (Lord of the Sabbath)
    - **13** **Parables of the kingdom** (sower, tares, mustard seed, leaven, treasure, pearl, net)
- **14-18** Glory and shadow: Christ rejected and misunderstood
    - **14** John beheaded; feeding of 5,000; walking on water
    - **15** Tradition of the elders; faith of a Canaanite woman; feeding of 4,000
    - **16** Caesarea Philippi confession; "I will build my church"; keys of the kingdom; Peter rebuked
    - **17** Transfiguration; healing of a boy with seizures
    - **18** Life under kingdom authority
        - Become like children (1-6)
        - Temptations to sin (7-9)
        - Leaving the 99 (10-14)
        - A brother who sins (15-20)
        - "Seventy-seven times"; parable of the unforgiving servant (21-35)
- **19-25** Opposition and eschatology: the triumph of grace
    - **19-20** Demands on disciples (divorce; rich young man; laborers in the vineyard)
    - **21** Triumphal entry; temple cleansing; parables (two sons, tenants)
    - **22** Wedding feast; controversies in the temple courts; the great commandment
    - **23** Woes on the scribes and Pharisees; lament over Jerusalem
    - **24-25** **Olivet Discourse**
        - **24** Birth pains; destruction of the temple; coming of the Son of Man
        - **25** Parables (ten virgins, talents); sheep and goats
- **26-28** Passion and resurrection
    - **26** Last Supper; Gethsemane; betrayal; denial
    - **27** Jesus before Pilate; crucifixion and mocking; death
    - **28** Resurrection and **Great Commission** (28:18-20)

### Key Passages

- **Beatitudes:** 5:3-12
- **Hell:** 5:22, 29-30; 10:28; 25:41, 46
- **Divorce:** 5:31-32; 19:3-9
- **Lord's Prayer:** 6:9-13
- **6:25** "Therefore I tell you, do not be anxious about your life, what you will eat or what you will drink, nor about your body, what you will put on."
- **7:7** "Ask, and it will be given to you; seek, and you will find; knock, and it will be opened to you."
- **11:28-30** "Come to me, all who labor and are heavy laden, and I will give you rest. Take my yoke upon you, and learn from me, for I am gentle and lowly in heart..."
- **16:18** "You are Peter, and on this rock I will build my church, and the gates of hell shall not prevail against it."
- **18:15** "If your brother sins against you, go and tell him his fault, between you and him alone."
- **22:37-40** "You shall love the Lord your God with all your heart and with all your soul and with all your mind... And a second is like it: You shall love your neighbor as yourself."
- **28:18-20** "All authority in heaven and on earth has been given to me. Go therefore and make disciples of all nations, baptizing them in the name of the Father and of the Son and of the Holy Spirit, teaching them to observe all that I have commanded you..."

### Sermon on the Mount (5-7)

- **5:1-2** Setting
- **5:3-12** The kingdom introduced, and its norms (Beatitudes)
- **5:13-16** The kingdom's witness (salt and light)
- **5:17-7:12** Body of the sermon
    - **5:17-48** The kingdom and "the Law and the Prophets" ("You have heard... but I say to you")
    - **6:1-18** Warning against hypocrisy: almsgiving, prayer, fasting
    - **6:19-34** Kingdom perspectives
        - Loyalty to kingdom values (treasures in heaven, the eye as lamp, two masters)
        - Trust in God: "Do not be anxious..."; "seek first the kingdom" (6:33)
    - **7:1-12** Judging, asking, the Golden Rule (7:12)
- **7:13-27** Conclusion
    - **13-14** Two ways
    - **15-20** Two trees
    - **21-23** Two claims
    - **24-27** Two builders

## Mark (16)

*Jesus, the Suffering Servant and Son of God*

**Key verses:** "The beginning of the gospel of Jesus Christ, the Son of God" (1:1); "For even the Son of Man came not to be served but to serve, and to give his life as a ransom for many" (**10:45**).

- **1:1-13** Prologue: John the Baptist; baptism; temptation
- **1:14-8:26** Who is this man? Ministry in Galilee
    - **1-3** Authority over disease, demons, and sin (paralytic, **2**); the Twelve chosen (**3**)
    - **4** Parables (sower, seed growing, mustard seed); calming the storm
    - **5** Gerasene demoniac; Jairus' daughter
    - **6** Rejection at Nazareth; the Twelve sent; feeding of 5,000; walking on water
    - **7-8:26** Clean and unclean; Syrophoenician woman; feeding of 4,000; the disciples' blindness
- **8:27-10:52** On the way to the cross: discipleship
    - **8:27-30** Peter's confession at Caesarea Philippi: **the turning point**
    - Three passion predictions (8:31; 9:31; 10:33-34), each followed by the disciples' misunderstanding and teaching on servanthood
    - **8:34** "Deny himself and take up his cross"
    - **9** Transfiguration
    - **10:45** The Son of Man came to serve and give his life as a ransom
- **11-16** Passion Week in Jerusalem
    - **11-12** Triumphal entry; temple cleansed; controversies
    - **13** Olivet Discourse
    - **14** Anointing at Bethany; Last Supper; Gethsemane; arrest; Peter's denial
    - **15** Trial before Pilate; crucifixion; the centurion: "Truly this man was the Son of God!" (15:39)
    - **16:1-8** The empty tomb (16:9-20 is not in the earliest manuscripts)

### Themes

- The messianic secret ("tell no one")
- Discipleship means following the Servant to the cross
- Action and urgency ("immediately," over 40 times)

## Luke (24)

*Jesus, the Son of Man, Came to Seek and to Save the Lost (19:10)*

- **1:1-4** Prologue: an "orderly account" for Theophilus
- **1:5-2:52** Birth and childhood
    - **1** Announcements to Zechariah and Mary; Magnificat (1:46-55); Benedictus (1:68-79)
    - **2** Birth in Bethlehem; **shepherds** (2:1-20); Simeon and Anna; the boy Jesus in the temple
- **3:1-4:13** Preparation: John the Baptist; baptism; genealogy to Adam; temptation
- **4:14-9:50** Ministry in Galilee
    - **4:16-30** Sermon at Nazareth (Isa. 61) and rejection
    - **5-6** Disciples called; the Twelve; Sermon on the Plain (6:20-49)
    - **7** Centurion's servant; widow of Nain's son; a sinful woman forgiven
    - **8** Parable of the sower; storm; demoniac; Jairus' daughter
    - **9:1-50** The Twelve sent; feeding of 5,000; Peter's confession; transfiguration
- **9:51-19:27** Journey to Jerusalem ("he set his face to go," 9:51)
    - **10** The seventy-two sent; **Good Samaritan** (10:25-37); **Mary and Martha** (10:38-42)
    - **11** The Lord's Prayer; teaching on prayer
    - **12-14** Rich fool; watchfulness; great banquet; the cost of discipleship
    - **15** Lost sheep, lost coin, **prodigal son**
    - **16** Shrewd manager; rich man and Lazarus
    - **17-18** Ten lepers; persistent widow; Pharisee and tax collector; rich ruler
    - **19:1-10** Zacchaeus ("the Son of Man came to seek and to save the lost")
    - **19:11-27** Parable of the minas
- **19:28-24:53** Jerusalem: death and resurrection
    - **19:28-21:38** Triumphal entry; temple teaching; Olivet Discourse
    - **22** Last Supper ("this cup... is the new covenant in my blood," 22:20); Gethsemane; arrest
    - **23** Trials before Pilate and Herod; crucifixion; the penitent thief
    - **24** Empty tomb; **road to Emmaus** ("beginning with Moses and all the Prophets," 24:27); commission; **ascension** (24:50-53)

### Themes

- Salvation for all: Gentiles, Samaritans, the poor, women, sinners
- The Holy Spirit, prayer, and joy
- Fulfillment of the OT (24:44)

## John (21)

*"That You May Believe that Jesus Is the Christ, the Son of God" (20:31)*

- **1:1-18** Prologue: the Word made flesh ("In the beginning was the Word," 1:1; "the Word became flesh and dwelt among us," 1:14)
- **1:19-12:50** **The Book of Signs:** public ministry
    - **1:19-51** John's witness ("Behold, the Lamb of God," 1:29); first disciples
    - **2** Water into wine (**sign 1**); temple cleansed
    - **3** Nicodemus: "You must be born again"; **3:16**
    - **4** Woman at the well; official's son healed (**sign 2**)
    - **5** Healing at the pool of Bethesda (**sign 3**); the Son's equality with the Father
    - **6** Feeding of 5,000 (**sign 4**); walking on water (**sign 5**); "I am the bread of life"
    - **7-8** Feast of Booths: living water (7:37-39); "I am the light of the world"; "before Abraham was, I am" (8:58)
    - **9** Man born blind healed (**sign 6**)
    - **10** The Good Shepherd; "I and the Father are one" (10:30)
    - **11** Lazarus raised (**sign 7**): "I am the resurrection and the life"
    - **12** Anointing at Bethany; triumphal entry; Greeks seek Jesus; Israel's unbelief
- **13-17** **The Upper Room (Farewell) Discourse**
    - **13** Foot washing; the new commandment (13:34-35); Judas goes out
    - **14** "I am the way, and the truth, and the life" (14:6); the Comforter promised
    - **15** The vine and the branches; the world's hatred
    - **16** The Spirit's work (16:7-15); sorrow turned to joy
    - **17** **High Priestly Prayer**
- **18-20** **The Book of Glory:** passion and resurrection
    - **18-19** Arrest; trials before Annas, Caiaphas, and Pilate; crucifixion ("It is finished," 19:30); burial
    - **20** Empty tomb; Mary Magdalene; Thomas: "My Lord and my God!" (20:28); purpose statement (20:30-31)
- **21** Epilogue: miraculous catch; Peter restored ("Feed my sheep")

### Seven Signs and Seven "I Am" Sayings

| Sign | Ch. | "I am..." | Ref. |
|---|---|---|---|
| Water into wine | 2 | the bread of life | 6:35 |
| Official's son healed | 4 | the light of the world | 8:12 |
| Paralytic at Bethesda | 5 | the door of the sheep | 10:7, 9 |
| Feeding of 5,000 | 6 | the good shepherd | 10:11, 14 |
| Walking on water | 6 | the resurrection and the life | 11:25 |
| Man born blind | 9 | the way, the truth, and the life | 14:6 |
| Lazarus raised | 11 | the true vine | 15:1 |

Plus the absolute "I am" (*ego eimi*): 8:24, 28, 58; 18:5-6.
