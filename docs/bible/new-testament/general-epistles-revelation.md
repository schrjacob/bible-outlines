# General Epistles & Revelation

!!! info "Exam level"
    **Outlines:** Hebrews and Revelation. **Summaries:** James, 1-2 Peter, 1-3 John, Jude.

## Hebrews (13)

*The Supremacy of Christ: Better than All That Came Before, So Hold Fast*

The author is unknown (Paul, Apollos, and Barnabas have been suggested). Written to Jewish Christians tempted to drift back to Judaism, before AD 70 (the temple service is still running). Key word: **better** (better than the angels, Moses, Aaron; a better covenant, sacrifice, hope).

- **1:1-4:13** Christ superior to the prophets, angels, and Moses
    - **1** "Long ago... God spoke... by the prophets, but in these last days he has spoken to us by his Son" (1:1-2); superior to angels
    - **2** *Warning 1: drifting* (2:1-4); the Son made like his brothers to be a merciful high priest
    - **3-4** Superior to Moses; *Warning 2: unbelief* (3:7-4:13); a Sabbath rest remains; "the word of God is living and active" (4:12)
- **4:14-10:18** Christ's superior priesthood, covenant, and sacrifice
    - **4:14-5:10** A great high priest who sympathizes with our weakness
    - **5:11-6:20** *Warning 3: immaturity and apostasy* (6:4-6); hope as an anchor
    - **7** A priest after the order of **Melchizedek** (Gen. 14; Ps. 110:4)
    - **8** A better covenant: the new covenant of Jer. 31 quoted
    - **9:1-10:18** A better sacrifice: once for all, by his own blood
- **10:19-13:25** Exhortations: persevere by faith
    - **10:19-39** Draw near; hold fast; "**not neglecting to meet together**" (10:24-25); *Warning 4: willful sin* (10:26-39)
    - **11** The "hall of faith": "faith is the assurance of things hoped for" (11:1); "without faith it is impossible to please him" (11:6)
    - **12** Run the race, looking to Jesus; **the Lord disciplines those he loves** (12:5-11); *Warning 5: refusing him who speaks* (12:25-29); Sinai vs. Zion
    - **13** Practical exhortations: hospitality, marriage, contentment; "Jesus Christ is the same yesterday and today and forever" (13:8); obey your leaders (13:17)

## James (5)

*A Living Faith that Produces Works.* Probably the earliest NT book, written by James the Lord's brother to "the twelve tribes in the Dispersion."

- **1** Trials produce steadfastness; ask God for wisdom (1:5); be doers of the word, not hearers only (1:22)
- **2** No partiality; "faith apart from works is dead" (2:17, 26): James addresses the *evidence* of justifying faith, Paul its *basis*
- **3** **Taming the tongue**; wisdom from above
- **4** Worldliness; "you do not have, because you do not ask... you ask wrongly" (**unanswered prayer**, 4:2-3); "if the Lord wills"
- **5** Warning to the rich; patience; prayer for the sick; call the elders (5:14-16)

## 1 Peter (5)

*Hope and Holiness amid Suffering.* To "elect exiles" scattered in Asia Minor.

- **1:1-2:10** Our identity: born again to a living hope (1:3); "be holy, for I am holy"; **living stones**, "a chosen race, a royal priesthood" (2:4-10)
- **2:11-4:11** Our conduct as exiles: submission to government, masters, and husbands (2:13-3:7); Christ our example in suffering (2:21-25); always ready to give a defense (3:15)
- **4:12-5:14** Suffering as Christians; shepherd the flock willingly (5:1-4: elders); "cast all your anxieties on him" (5:7); the devil a roaring lion

## 2 Peter (3)

*Grow in Grace; Beware False Teachers; the Lord Will Come.* Peter's last testament.

- **1** Make your calling and election sure; eyewitness of the transfiguration; Scripture from men "carried along by the Holy Spirit" (1:21)
- **2** False teachers and their judgment
- **3** Scoffers; the Lord is "not wishing that any should perish" (3:9); the day of the Lord; new heavens and earth; Paul's letters called Scripture (3:16)

## 1 John (5)

*Assurance of Eternal Life.* "I write these things to you... **that you may know** that you have eternal life" (**5:13**). Written against early Gnostic and docetic teaching that denied Christ came in the flesh.

- Tests of true faith, cycled through the letter:
    - **Doctrinal:** confessing that Jesus is the Christ come in the flesh (2:22; 4:2-3)
    - **Moral:** walking in the light; keeping his commandments (1:5-2:6)
    - **Social:** loving the brothers ("God is love," 4:8, 16)
- "If we confess our sins, he is faithful and just to forgive us" (1:9); Christ our advocate and propitiation (2:1-2)

## 2 John (1)

*Walk in Truth and Love; Refuse Hospitality to Deceivers.* To "the **elect lady** and her children" (a church): do not receive anyone who denies that Jesus Christ came in the flesh.

## 3 John (1)

*Commending Gaius for supporting traveling missionaries,* and rebuking Diotrephes, "who likes to put himself first."

## Jude (1)

*Contend for the Faith against Apostates.* "Contend for the faith that was once for all delivered to the saints" (v. 3). Examples of judgment from the OT; Michael and the devil; Enoch's prophecy; the doxology "to him who is able to keep you from stumbling" (vv. 24-25).

## Revelation (22)

*The Lamb Has Conquered: Christ's Victory and the New Creation*

Written by John, exiled on Patmos (c. AD 95), to seven churches in Asia under persecution. Structure key (1:19): "the things that you have seen [**1**], those that are [**2-3**], and those that are to take place after this [**4-22**]."

- **1** Vision of the glorified Son of Man among the lampstands
- **2-3** Letters to seven churches: **Ephesus** (lost first love), **Smyrna** (suffering), **Pergamum** (compromise), **Thyatira** (Jezebel), **Sardis** (dead), **Philadelphia** (open door), **Laodicea** (lukewarm)
- **4-5** The throne room: God the Creator worshiped; the Lamb who was slain is worthy to open the scroll
- **6:1-8:5** Seven seals (the four horsemen; the 144,000 and the great multitude, **7**)
- **8:6-11:19** Seven trumpets (the two witnesses, **11**)
- **12-14** The cosmic conflict: the woman, the dragon, the two beasts (666); the Lamb on Mt. Zion
- **15-16** Seven bowls of wrath
- **17-19** The fall of Babylon the great; the marriage supper of the Lamb; the Rider on the white horse
- **20** The thousand years (**millennium**); Satan bound and released; the **great white throne** judgment (20:11-15)
- **21-22** New heaven and new earth; the New Jerusalem ("he will wipe away every tear," 21:4); the river and tree of life; "Come, Lord Jesus!" (22:20)

!!! note "Interpretive approaches"
    - **Preterist:** fulfilled mainly in the first century (the fall of Jerusalem or Rome)
    - **Historicist:** a map of church history from the first century to the end
    - **Futurist:** chapters 4-22 are mostly still future
    - **Idealist:** timeless symbols of the conflict between Christ and Satan

    Many Reformed interpreters combine the idealist approach with **progressive parallelism** (William Hendriksen, *More Than Conquerors*): seven parallel sections, each spanning the period from Christ's first coming to his second.

!!! note "Millennial views (Rev. 20:1-10)"
    - **Amillennial:** the "1,000 years" is the present church age; Christ reigns now and Satan is bound from deceiving the nations. This is the majority Reformed view.
    - **Postmillennial:** the gospel will bring a long era of righteousness and peace before Christ returns.
    - **Historic premillennial:** Christ returns, then reigns for 1,000 years on earth.
    - **Dispensational premillennial:** a pre-tribulation rapture; a distinct future for national Israel.

    The PCA does not make a millennial position a test of orthodoxy. The Westminster Standards teach one general resurrection and judgment at Christ's return (WCF 32-33).
