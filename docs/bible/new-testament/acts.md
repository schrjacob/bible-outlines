# Acts

!!! info "Exam level: full outline"
    Chapell expects an **outline of Acts** and asks: *"What are the basic elements found in each of the sermons recorded in Acts?"* Key passages to locate include Pentecost, Paul's conversion, the Jerusalem Council, the missionary journeys, the Philippian jailer, the Bereans, and Apollos.

## Acts (28)

*The Risen Christ Builds His Church by the Spirit, from Jerusalem to Rome*

**Key verse (and outline):** "You will receive power when the Holy Spirit has come upon you, and you will be my witnesses in **Jerusalem** [1-7] and in all **Judea and Samaria** [8-12], and to **the end of the earth** [13-28]" (**1:8**). It can also be outlined by its main characters: **Peter** (1-12) and **Paul** (13-28).

- **1-7** Witness in Jerusalem
    - **1** Ascension (1:9-11); waiting and prayer; Matthias chosen
    - **2** **Pentecost**: the Spirit poured out (Joel 2); Peter's sermon; 3,000 baptized; the life of the church (2:42-47)
    - **3-4** Lame man healed; Peter's sermon in Solomon's Portico; arrest; "no other name under heaven" (4:12)
    - **5** Ananias and Sapphira; apostles arrested and freed; Gamaliel's counsel; "we must obey God rather than men" (5:29)
    - **6** Seven chosen to serve tables (the first deacons)
    - **7** Stephen's speech and martyrdom (the first martyr); Saul approves
- **8-12** Witness in Judea and Samaria
    - **8** Persecution scatters the church; Philip in Samaria; Simon the magician; the Ethiopian eunuch
    - **9** **Conversion of Saul** on the Damascus road; Ananias; Peter heals Aeneas and raises Dorcas
    - **10-11** **Cornelius**: the gospel to the Gentiles; Peter's vision; the Spirit falls; the church at Antioch ("first called Christians," 11:26)
    - **12** James (son of Zebedee) killed by Herod Agrippa I; Peter freed from prison; Herod's death
- **13-28** Witness to the end of the earth
    - **13-14** **First missionary journey** (Barnabas and Paul)
    - **15:1-35** **Jerusalem Council**: Gentiles are saved by grace without circumcision
    - **15:36-18:22** **Second missionary journey** (Paul and Silas; Timothy joins; into Europe)
    - **18:23-21:16** **Third missionary journey** (Ephesus; Apollos, 18:24-28; farewell to the Ephesian elders at Miletus, 20:17-38)
    - **21:17-26:32** Arrest in Jerusalem; defenses before the crowd (22), the Sanhedrin (23), Felix (24), Festus (25), and Agrippa (26); appeal to Caesar
    - **27-28** Voyage, shipwreck on Malta; Rome: Paul preaches "with all boldness and without hindrance" (28:31)

### Progress Reports

Luke marks the growth of the church with summary statements: "the word of God continued to increase" (**6:7; 9:31; 12:24; 16:5; 19:20; 28:30-31**).

### Themes

- The Holy Spirit empowering witness
- The resurrection as the heart of the message
- God's sovereignty over opposition and persecution
- Jew and Gentile united in one church
- Continuity: what Jesus "began to do and teach" (1:1) he continues through his church

## The Sermons of Acts

**Basic elements** (the apostolic *kerygma*, following C. H. Dodd):

1. **Fulfillment:** the age of fulfillment has dawned, as the prophets foretold.
2. **The facts:** this happened through the ministry, death, and resurrection of Jesus, the son of David.
3. **Exaltation:** by the resurrection Jesus has been exalted to God's right hand as Lord and Christ.
4. **The Spirit:** the Holy Spirit in the church is the sign of Christ's present power and glory.
5. **Consummation:** Christ will return as Judge.
6. **Appeal:** repent and believe, with the promise of forgiveness and the gift of the Spirit.

Memory aid: **Prophecy, Person, Passion, Resurrection, Return, Repent.** Apostolic preaching always includes eyewitness testimony ("we are witnesses") and Scripture proof.
