# Whole Bible

<!-- cols:1 continuous -->

Chapell, section A. These are often the opening questions of the Bible exam: easy points, so have them cold.

| Question | Answer |
|---|---|
| How many books are in the Bible? | **66**: **39** in the Old Testament and **27** in the New Testament |
| Have you read the entire Bible? | Be ready to answer honestly, and to say how you read it (plan, frequency) |
| Where in the OT are the Ten Commandments? (two references) | **Exodus 20:1-17** and **Deuteronomy 5:6-21** |
| Where in the NT is the law summarized? (two references, quote one) | **Matthew 22:37-40** (also Mark 12:29-31; Luke 10:27) and **Romans 13:8-10** (also Gal. 5:14; James 2:8) |
| Two passages about the revelation of God in nature | **Psalm 19:1-6** and **Romans 1:18-20** (also Acts 14:15-17; 17:24-28) |

### Quotations to Have Ready

- **Matthew 22:37-40:** "You shall love the Lord your God with all your heart and with all your soul and with all your mind. This is the great and first commandment. And a second is like it: You shall love your neighbor as yourself. On these two commandments depend all the Law and the Prophets."
- **Romans 13:10:** "Love does no wrong to a neighbor; therefore love is the fulfilling of the law."
- **Psalm 19:1:** "The heavens declare the glory of God, and the sky above proclaims his handiwork."
- **Romans 1:20:** "For his invisible attributes, namely, his eternal power and divine nature, have been clearly perceived, ever since the creation of the world, in the things that have been made. So they are without excuse."

## The Bible's Storyline

**Creation > Fall > Redemption > Consummation**

| Era | Books | Summary |
|---|---|---|
| Beginnings | Gen. 1-11 | Creation, Fall, Flood, Babel |
| Patriarchs | Gen. 12-50 | Abraham, Isaac, Jacob, Joseph: the promise |
| Exodus and law | Exodus - Deuteronomy | Redemption from Egypt; covenant at Sinai; wilderness |
| Conquest and judges | Joshua - Ruth | The land given; cycles of apostasy |
| Kingdom | Samuel, Kings, Chronicles | Saul, David, Solomon; division; decline (with the prophets) |
| Exile | (Kings, Jer., Ezek., Dan.) | Israel to Assyria (722); Judah to Babylon (586) |
| Return | Ezra, Nehemiah, Esther, Haggai, Zechariah, Malachi | Temple and walls rebuilt; waiting for the Messiah |
| Silence | 400 years | Persia > Greece > Rome |
| Christ | Gospels | Incarnation, ministry, death, resurrection, ascension |
| Church | Acts, Epistles | The Spirit poured out; the gospel to the nations |
| Consummation | Revelation | Christ returns; new heavens and new earth |
