## How Chapell Says to Prepare for the Bible Exam

- **Book outlines:** show you grasp the overall content of the book and can *locate key events*. Chapell recommends a **"telescoping" outline**: a few main divisions, any of which you can expand. Include chapter divisions (verses for significant cases).
- **Book contents:** summarize briefly (*"even in a word or phrase"*) the contents of **every** book.
- **Bible content questions:** sections A-I, which cover the whole Bible, OT and NT people, passages, events, and topics.

## If Time Is Short: Study in Tiers

<!-- docx-skip -->
On the website, use the **All / Tier 1 / Tier 2 / Tier 3** buttons at the bottom of any page to show just that tier. The choice carries from page to page.

<!-- /docx-skip -->
!!! success "Tier 1: know cold (most likely to be asked; easiest points)"
    1. **66 / 39 / 27**; the OT and NT divisions and the books in order ([Whole Bible](whole-bible.md))
    2. **A one-line summary of every book** (the OT and NT overview pages)
    3. **Anchor dates:** 2000, 1446, 1000, 931, 722, 586, 538, 516
    4. **The covenants** with their key passages (Gen. 3:15; 12/15/17; Ex. 19-24; 2 Sam. 7; Jer. 31)
    5. **Chapell's 60 NT passages** and the **OT key passages and events** (locate by book and chapter)
    6. **Top-level outlines** (main divisions only) of Genesis, Exodus, Matthew, John, Acts, and Romans

!!! warning "Tier 2: second pass"
    1. Top-level outlines of the other outline books: Leviticus, Numbers, Deuteronomy, Isaiah, Jeremiah, Ezekiel, Daniel, Mark, Luke, 1-2 Corinthians, Galatians, Ephesians, Philippians, Colossians, Hebrews, Revelation
    2. Key **people** (Chapell's starred names) and the **30 NT topics**
    3. **Paul's journeys** and which letters go with them; elements of the **Acts sermons**
    4. **Gospel distinctives**; three **parables** and three **miracles**

!!! note "Tier 3: if time allows"
    Second-level detail in the outlines, the Psalms catalog, the Levitical feasts, the minor-prophet structures, places.

### Method

- **Telescope:** learn level 1 of every outline before going to level 2. A three-point outline of every book beats a twelve-point outline of a few.
- **Say it out loud.** Exams are often oral. Practice answering in one crisp sentence, then add detail only if asked.
- **Quiz in both directions:** "Where is the Jerusalem Council?" and "What happens in Acts 15?"
- **Overlap with theology:** the NT topics page doubles as a proof-text list for the theology exam.

## Hot Topics (Chapell)

Not part of the Bible exam, but be ready to state and defend your views: creation, charismatic gifts, the regulative principle, Sabbath, the role of women in the church, re-baptism, theonomy, civil disobedience, paedo-communion, divorce and remarriage, confessional subscription, and fencing the Lord's Table.
