# Ordination Study Guide

A study guide for the examinations for licensure and ordination in the Presbyterian Church in America (PCA). It follows the five examination areas and the questions in Bryan Chapell and Jim Meek, *Preparing for Licensure and Ordination Exams* (PCA Christian Education and Publications, 2002).

<div class="grid cards exam-cards" markdown>

-   :material-book-open-page-variant: **[Bible](bible/index.md)**

    Outlines and summaries of all 66 books, the whole-Bible questions, Old and New Testament people, passages, events, and topics.

-   :material-school-outline: **[Theology](theology/index.md)**

    Chapell's theology questions in the order of the Westminster Confession, with proofs, the Five Points, the ordo salutis, and a gospel outline.

-   :material-gavel: **[Church Government](church-government/index.md)**

    The Book of Church Order: principles, officers, courts, calling and ordination, discipline, review and control, and the Directory for Worship.

-   :material-bank-outline: **[Church History](church-history/index.md)**

    Denominations, events, definitions, and 41 people, plus the history of Presbyterianism and of the PCA.

-   :material-water-outline: **[Sacraments](sacraments/index.md)**

    Baptism and the Lord's Supper: meaning, mode, subjects, the Reformed view among the others, and the practical cases.

</div>

## What the PCA Requires

| | Licensure (BCO 19-2) | Ordination (BCO 21-4) |
|---|---|---|
| Christian experience and call | Written and/or oral | Experimental religion, character, family (1 Tim. 3; Titus 1) |
| **Bible** | "Practical knowledge of Bible content" | "Bible content" |
| **Theology** | Biblical doctrine per the Confession and Catechisms | "Theology" |
| **Sacraments** | (within biblical doctrine) | "Sacraments" |
| **Church government** | Basic knowledge of the BCO | Principles and rules of government and discipline |
| **Church history** | (not required) | Church history; the history of the PCA |
| Other | Sermon | Greek and Hebrew; thesis; exegesis; sermon |

Read all of BCO 19 and 21 for the processes and deadlines.

## How to Use This Guide

1. Pick the exam tab you are preparing for. Each opens with how Chapell says to prepare, a map of his questions, and a **tiered study plan**.
2. Use the **All / Tier 1 / Tier 2 / Tier 3** buttons at the bottom of any page to show just that tier of content. The choice carries from page to page.
3. Use the tables as flashcards: cover the right-hand column and answer aloud. Exams are usually oral.
4. Use the search box (press ++s++ or ++slash++) to jump to any book, doctrine, person, or BCO topic.
5. Each tab has its own color so you always know which exam you are in.

!!! warning "Verify the details that change"
    The Book of Church Order is amended nearly every year, and Assembly decisions and your presbytery's own policies matter. Treat the Church Government and PCA-history pages as study guides, and check the specific vote counts, deadlines, and procedures against the current BCO and your presbytery's manual.
