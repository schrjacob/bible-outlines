# Bible Outlines for PCA Ordination Exams

A study guide for the **Bible content** and **theology** portions of licensure and ordination exams in the Presbyterian Church in America. It follows the requirements in Bryan Chapell and Jim Meek, *Preparing for Licensure and Ordination Exams*.

<div class="grid cards" markdown>

-   :material-map-check-outline: **[Exam Guide](exam-guide.md)**

    What the presbytery expects, which books need outlines and which need only summaries, and a tiered plan if time is short.

-   :material-book-open-variant: **[Whole Bible](whole-bible.md)**

    66 / 39 / 27, the Ten Commandments, the law summarized, general revelation, and the storyline.

-   :material-school-outline: **[Theology](theology/index.md)**

    Chapell's theology questions, answered in the order of the Westminster Confession, with proofs, the Five Points, the ordo salutis, and a gospel outline.

-   :material-script-text-outline: **[Old Testament](old-testament/index.md)**

    Book outlines and summaries, the timeline and covenants, people, places, passages, and events.

-   :material-cross-outline: **[New Testament](new-testament/index.md)**

    The Gospels, the life of Christ, Acts, Paul's journeys, the epistles, Revelation, and Chapell's 60 passages and 30 topics.

</div>

## How to Use This Guide

1. Start with the **[Exam Guide](exam-guide.md)** and its **Tier 1** list.
2. For each outline book, learn the **bold chapter divisions** first, and expand only once every book has its top level.
3. Use the **[NT Key Passages](new-testament/passages.md)** and **[OT Passages & Events](old-testament/passages-events.md)** tables as flashcards: cover the right column.
4. Use the search box (press ++s++ or ++slash++) to jump to any book, person, or passage.
